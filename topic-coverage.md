## What to practise next
1. **Essays:** take your weekly timed essays from the gaps first: crime & punishment, transport, advertising, social media, aging population, food & diet, science & space. These are among the most common themes and you have no practice or topic vocabulary for them.
2. **Letters:** take a break from work letters, which are a third of your letters. Do the missing types first, starting with asking for information, recommending someone and asking a friend for a favor. Write more informal letters too: you've done 9 informal against 31 formal or semi-formal, and informal tone is where several of your letters slipped.
3. **Repeats:** letters 08/09 and essays 08/09 are the same prompts done twice. That's fine as a rewrite exercise, but it inflates the counts for those topics.
