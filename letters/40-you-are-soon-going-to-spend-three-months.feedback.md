**Verdict.** This is closer to S5 1B Script C (Band 6) than to the Band 7 letters. The situation is changed from three months of work experience to an AI "training program" or "course", the format slips in the opening and closing, and the linking is essay-style. It is a full band below engnovate because CC and GRA 8 are not supported: the linkers are mechanical and the article errors recur.

- **Task Achievement:** All three bullets are present, but each is brief, and they describe a course, not work experience in an organisation. "Hello Mr Jhonson" is too casual for the register and "In conclusion" is essay-style: minor inconsistencies in tone and format (TA 6). The missing name after "Best regards" is not penalised. At exactly 150 words it only just meets the minimum.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphs are logical, but "That being said" (a contrast marker) introduces a consequence. "Additionally / First / And second / In conclusion" reads as a mechanical chain. "It is rapidly evolving…" has an unclear referent.
- **Lexical Resource:** "grasp the core principles" and "in the long run" are good choices. There are 2 spelling errors (Jhonson, aquiring), and "unique opportunity" and "contemporary world" are clichés.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 6 of 8 sentences are error-free, but the range is modest, and the article errors recur ("rapidly evolving … technology", "a specific AI tools").

**Key corrections**
- "Hello Mr Jhonson," → "Dear Mr Johnson," — formal salutation and spelling.
- "to participate in the newest AI training program" → "to spend three months gaining work experience in your organisation" — match the task.
- "It is rapidly evolving and extremely promising technology" → "This is a rapidly evolving and extremely promising technology" — article and clear referent.
- "That being said, I expect that aquiring" → "I therefore expect that acquiring" — linker and spelling.
- "a specific AI tools" → "specific AI tools" — article with a plural noun.
- "In conclusion, … Best regards" → "Thank you once again … Yours sincerely, [full name]" — letter format.

**To reach Band 7:** Keep to the prompt's situation (a work placement), and ask two or three practical questions (hours, supervisor, dress code, tasks), each with a reason.
