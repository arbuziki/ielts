**Verdict.** This is nowhere near the S9 Band 9 model, which wins on clear, consistent facts and plain accuracy. The letter contradicts itself on its key fact, and it has collocation and tense errors. engnovate's 9s ignore "errors extremely rare" (trap #4).

- **Task Achievement:** The purpose is clear and the requests (a transaction history, canceling the fee) are specific. However, "I didn't commit any transaction since then" clashes with "the last purchase I made yesterday", which counts as inaccurate detail. The $240 is never addressed, and making the cancellation conditional ("Should it be confirmed…") is weak when you know the bank is wrong. The contraction "didn't" slips in the formal register (Band 6).
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Reference is good ("this inaccuracy", "the account in question"), but "I would like to" appears 4 times and "on your side" twice. The contradiction also weakens the logic.
- **Lexical Resource:** "root cause" and "deducted" are precise. "commit a transaction" (used twice) is a wrong collocation, and "monetary amount" is redundant. There are no spelling errors.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 3 of 5 sentences are error-free. There is a present-perfect error with "since then" and an article error ("the desynchronization").

**Key corrections**
- "I didn't commit any transaction since then" → "I have not made any transactions since then" — collocation, tense and register.
- "the monetary amount has been refunded" → "the amount has been refunded" — redundant.
- "related to the desynchronization in your system" → "caused by a synchronization error in your system" — article and naturalness.
- "transactions committed using the account" → "transactions made on the account" — collocation.
- "Should it be confirmed … I would like to inquire whether it would be possible to cancel the penalty." → "I therefore ask you to cancel the $70 charge and correct my balance." — firmer request.

**To reach Band 8:** Fix the timeline so the facts agree (the refunded purchase, then the correct balance), quote the $240 and $70 figures, and state your demand directly.
