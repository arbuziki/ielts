## Prompt

WRITING TASK 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

Last month you had a holiday overseas where you stayed with some friends.
They have just sent you some photos of your holiday.

Write a letter to your friends. In your letter
• thank them for the photos and for the holiday
• explain why you didn't write earlier
• invite them to come and stay with you

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ................. ,

## My answer

Hey guys,

I want to thank you all again for such an amazing trip and the pictures. The one where we're standing atop of the hill overlooking the valey, with the city lights flickering in the distance on the other side, is just breathtaking! I also thoroughly enjoyed the experience of staying at your place, you have a great house!

Sorry that I didn't get a chance to text you earlier, it's been such a hustle when I got back here. I had to catch up on a ton of my work assignments, since the backlog was piling up during my vacation.

But now, when I've got everything sorted out, it's actually my turn to invite you over! Even though, your country is just mesmerizing with its incredible nature, formidable mountains, and crystall clear lakes, mine is equally wonderful. This time around, you can stay at my place: I have enough room to accommodate everyone.

Take care,
