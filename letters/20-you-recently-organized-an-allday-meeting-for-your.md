## Prompt

You recently organized an all-day meeting for your company, which took place in a local hotel. In their feedback, participants at the meeting said that they liked the hotel, but they were unhappy about the food that was served for lunch.
Write a letter to the manager of the hotel. In your letter
Say what the participants liked about the hotel
Explain why they were unhappy about the food
Suggest what the manager should do to improve the food in the future

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing to provide my feedback regarding our experience of hosting a company event at your hotel. To my knowledge, the overall experience for our employees and guests has been overwhelmingly positive: I received emails commending spacious rooms, good soundproofing, and a well-equipped gym.

Having said that, I would like to highlight the area for potential improvement: I have received a number of complaints from our employees, claiming that the food choice was rather scarse, and some of the meals were stale.

Having looked into the organizational details, I found out that this problem might have been caused by the catering company, which was in charge of arranging meals. I humbly suggest that you might want to consider another, more reliable options in the future. Nonetheless, this mishap did impede our ability to enjoy your hospitality, and we are looking forward to the prospect of hosing another event at your hotel.

Sincerely yours,
