**Verdict.** The language is close to the S5 1B Script D Band 7 level, but the task is answered less fully than any Band 7+ anchor. The third bullet asks the manager for help arranging the move, and the letter only asks for expense reimbursement. Part of bullet 1 is a padded stock sentence. These two points explain the full band below engnovate.

- **Task Achievement:** Bullet 1 gives a real reason (landing a client, time zones), but the "Should I be granted this opportunity… significant asset" sentence is generic padding. The six-month duration is never stated. Bullet 2 is well handled (a colleague will cover, meetings rescheduled). Bullet 3 is narrowed to money, with no request for help with approval, visa or accommodation,. That gives TA 6.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The three paragraphs track the bullets, but "Meanwhile, during the time of my absence" is redundant and "Meanwhile" is misused. The ending is abrupt.
- **Lexical Resource:** "land a new major customer" is a good collocation. Against it: "communicative overhead", "immediate presence" (physical presence), "substitue me" and "we are aimed to". The template is discounted from range.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 3 of 6 sentences are error-free. The errors are mild but recurring tense and verb-form problems (arranged → have arranged; will → would). The structures are varied (inversion, *whether* clauses).

**Key corrections**
- "since we are aimed to land a new major customer" → "since we aim to win a major new client" — verb form
- "I arranged for my colleague to substitue me" → "I have arranged for a colleague to cover for me" — tense, spelling, verb pattern
- "I realized that I will be able to attend" → "I would be able to attend" — sequence of tenses
- "reduce communicative overhead" → "reduce communication delays" — collocation
- "my temporary relocation to the head office" → "a six-month secondment to the head office" — state the duration

**To reach Band 8:** Replace the stock "asset" sentence with a specific outcome. Make bullet 3 a real request ("Could you support my application to HR and help with the visa and housing?"), then close with a line about discussing it.
