## Prompt

WRITING TASK 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have recently moved to a different house.

Write a letter to an English-speaking friend. In your letter
• explain why you have moved
• describe the new house
• invite your friend to come and visit

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ............. ,

## My answer

Dear John,

I'm writing to let you know that I've moved in to a new place.

It's been a long time, but we finally got a chance to improve our living conditions. As you might have remembered, my previous place was incredibly crammed, and the neighborhood was in the middle of an industrial area. I was afraid that this chronic exposure to high levels of polluted air might result in some serious medical condition over time.

So, now I live in a modern residential apartment complex located in the outskirts of town right by the shore. My apartment is quite spacious with two bedrooms and a kitchen coming with this island table made out of marble that I've been always dreaming of! From my balcony, I look out over the park and the sea; the view is just breathtaking!

If you assumed that I'm just showing off my new place, that's exactly what I'm doing! But hey, you should come and see for yourself. I'm throwing a small party to celebrate and will be happy to see you!

Faithfully yours,
