**Verdict.** Below the S5 1B Script D Band 7 anchor. The benefit bullet is developed, but the company-help bullet is one sentence, and words chosen to impress are often imprecise or in the wrong register. engnovate's TA 8 over-credits a bare request, while its GRA 5 is too harsh.

- **Task Achievement:** All bullets are present, but bullet 1 has no course specifics (provider, length, cost, format). Bullet 2 is a single request with no amount and no alternative, so it is only adequately highlighted (TA 6). The last sentence is a stock template that adds nothing.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** One paragraph per bullet with clear progression. Some linkers are misused: "Namely" introduces examples, "such as … just to name a few" is redundant, and "On top of that" is informal.
- **Lexical Resource:** Some good items ("delving into the intricacies", "at the forefront"). There are also inappropriacies: "top-notch" and "a substantial chunk" are too informal, and "benefits … will be indispensable" is the wrong meaning. 3 spelling errors ("thechology", "presentiations", "opporunity").
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 5 of 8 sentences are error-free. The errors cluster in the complex structures (the inversion, a comma splice, "if is is").

**Key corrections**
- "if is is possible" → "if it is possible" (missing subject)
- "AI is at the forefront of technical progress, its immense impact is already seen" → "…technical progress, and its impact can already be seen" (comma splice)
- "Shall I get this opporunity to partake in this course, the benefits for the company will be indispensable." → "Should I be given this opportunity, I am confident the company would benefit considerably." (the inversion takes "Should"; "indispensable" means "essential")
- "alleviate a substantial chunk of my routine work" → "take over a large part of my routine work" (collocation; register)
- "Namely," → "In particular," (wrong linker)

**To reach Band 7:** Give hard facts for bullets 1–2: name the provider, length and cost (e.g. "a six-week evening course costing $900"). Ask for something specific, such as half the fee or two hours of study time a week. Replace the final template with one plain sentence.
