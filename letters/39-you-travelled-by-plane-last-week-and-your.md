## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing to draw your attention to an incident with my luggage, which happened last week during the flight to New York City: upon my arrival, my suitcase was nowhere to be found. I filed an official complaint at the comany's counter, but have not heard back from you.

I would like to describe the lost suitcase once again so that you could move forward with the search: it is a relatively small black bag made from genuine leather. My suitcase contained some premium clothing and perfume, from Hugo Boss t-shirts to Dior colonges. When acquiring additional insurance which covers lost luggage, I estimated the monetary value of these items as much as $10,000.

Given the circumstances, I would like to inquire about the timeline regarding the final response: if my suitcase has indeed been lost, I would expect the carrier to reimburse me for lost items in full.

Sincerely,
