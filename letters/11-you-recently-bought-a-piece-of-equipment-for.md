## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You recently bought a piece of equipment for your kitchen but it did not work. You phoned the shop but no action was taken.

Write a letter to the shop manager. In your letter
- describe the problem with the equipment
- explain what happened when you phoned the shop
- say what you would like the manager to do.

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:
Dear sir or Madam,

## My answer

Dear sir,

I am writing to formally file a complaint regarding a refrigerator I purchased from your store.

The refrigerator in question ceased operating just after a week of use. I only hear the noise coming from the compressor, but no actual cooling is happening. I am not sure what might have caused this issue, but a friend of mine, upon looking at the fridge, said that the freon might have been leaking due to an incorrect installation.

Having explained the problem to the clerk over the phone, I was assured that the company will have a specialist sent over to look into the issue. I was provided with the contact, but I have not heard from them ever since.

Given the circumstances, I have no other choice but claim a full refund in accordance with my two-year warranty. I would greately appreciate it if this process did not take longer than two weeks, as it is stated in the warranty. Otherwise, I would have to resort to take legal actions, including the involvment of my lawer.

Sincerely yours,
