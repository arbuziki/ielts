## Prompt

WRITING TASK 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have recently started work in a new company.

Write a letter to an English-speaking friend. In your letter
• explain why you changed jobs
• describe your new job
• tell him/her your other news

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ................. ,

## My answer

Dear John,

I'm writing to let you know that I have finally managed to secure a new job at a big tech company. As you might remember, I have been sifting through the job market for quite some time, since my previous employer skimped on my promotion. I was feeling as if I hit a glass ceiling, so I decided to quit. And after months of griding job interviews, my endeavor has finally paid off.

First and foremost, let me tell you a little about my new role: I'm a senior software developer in a cyber security department. I'm in charge of the development of a new top-notch algorithm that recognizes and prevents cyber attacks. Besides the fact that this role is extremely fascinating and challenging, I managed to negoctiate a generous compensation with the HR manager.

Look, if you think that I'm just showing off my new role, that's exactly what I'm doing. But hey, from what I've heard, they are still hiring, so send me your CV, and I'll pass it over to the manager. It think it would be wonderful if we got to work together like back in old good times.

Take care,
