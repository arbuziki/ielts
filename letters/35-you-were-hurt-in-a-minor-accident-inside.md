## Prompt

WRITING TASK 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You were hurt in a minor accident inside a supermarket, and you wish to complain to the supermarket.

Write a letter to the manager of the supermarket. In your letter
• say who you are
• give details about the accident
• suggest how the supermarket could prevent similar accidents.

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:
Dear Sir or Madam,

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am a regular visitor to one of your branches, which is located on the edge of town. I am mostly satisfied with the service and the variety of choice; nonetheless, I would like to draw your attention to a significant drawback that resulted in a minor injury for me.

The flaw in question is the layout of most of your stores: the narrow aisles between the shelves not only obstruct movement but aslo can lead to accidents, like the one that happened to me. While I was trying to reach for some item, I accidentally pushed bottles on the opposite shelf with my elbow, causing some of them to fall right on my head.

Given the discomfort that this crampend layout causes to customers, I would like to inquire whether the company would consider making any adjustments, such as widening the space between the shelves or rearranging the items by placing the heavies products at the bottom. I am convinced that such minor changes could drastically increase overall customer experience.

Sincerely,
