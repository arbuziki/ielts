**Verdict.** The content and flow are strong: bullets 1–2 are vivid and the letter is easy to follow. But the tone slips: "Faithfully yours" is wrong for a friend, and the clinical "chronic exposure" sentence is too formal. The invitation also has no details, and half the sentences contain a slip. That places it at Band 7, like S5 1A Script B. engnovate's 8.0 (TA 9) misses the register error, which trap #7 treats as a TA issue.

- **Task Achievement:** Bullets 1–2 are well extended. Bullet 3 is a bare invitation with no date, no details of the party and no offer to stay. With the formal sign-off and stiff medical register, this counts as "minor inconsistencies in tone", so TA 6.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Natural informal linking ("So, now", "But hey"), clear paragraphs and effective reference. The reader follows with ease.
- **Lexical Resource:** Lively and appropriate ("showing off", "look out over", "throwing a small party", "breathtaking"). But "crammed" should be "cramped", "a kitchen coming with this island table" is awkward, and "chronic exposure to high levels of polluted air" is the wrong register for a friend.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 5 of 10 sentences are error-free. Preposition errors recur ("moved in to", "in the outskirts"), and so do tense and aspect errors ("might have remembered", "I've been always dreaming", "If you assumed").

**Key corrections**
- "Faithfully yours," → "Take care, / Best wishes," plus your name (sign-off for a friend)
- "I've moved in to a new place" → "I've moved to a new place" (preposition)
- "As you might have remembered" → "As you might remember" (tense)
- "incredibly crammed" → "incredibly cramped" (word choice)
- "in the outskirts of town" → "on the outskirts of town" (preposition)
- "a kitchen coming with this island table made out of marble that I've been always dreaming of" → "a kitchen with the marble island I've always dreamed of" (word order; natural phrasing)

**To reach Band 8:** Use an informal sign-off and add invitation details, e.g. "the house-warming is on Saturday the 14th – stay the night, there's a spare room". Fix the preposition and tense slips.
