## Prompt

A friend of yours is thinking of going on a camping holiday for the first time this summer. He/She has asked for your advice.
Write a letter to your friend. In your letter:
Explain why you think your friend would enjoy a camping holiday
Describe some possible disadvantages
Say whether you would like to go camping with your friend this summer

## My answer

Dear John,

I just wanted to drop you a line to congrat you with a very brave decision to go on a sole weekend hiking trip for the first time! It's such a great idea to venture off into the wilderness, exploring picturesque landscapes while enjoying fresh air.

Having said that, you should also keep in mind the potential risks: possible exhaustion, lack of fresh water, wild animals. If I were you, I would thoroughly look into the details of this trail on online forums: other hikers tend to share tons of useful information, from the best spots to set up a camp, to the sources of water like streams or rivers.

It's such a bummer that I won't be able to join you on this trip! I'm totally bugged down in my backlog; besides, we're reaching the deadline in a very important project at my work. Nevertheless, I wish you all the best and will be rooting for you, dude!

Take care,
