## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You want to sell some of your furniture. You think a friend of yours might like to buy it from you.

Write a letter to your friend. In your letter

• explain why you are selling
• describe the furniture
• suggest a date when your friend can come and see the furniture

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ............ ,

## My answer

Dear John,

How have you been? I just wanted to drop you a line to say that you're in luck, as I just put up for sale my all-time favorite gaming chair for just half price. I would definitely keep it, but I'm moving into a new place in a few weeks, so I decided to sell out some of my most heavy stuff.

I'm talking about the exact chair that you've seen many times while visiting me: the top-tier gaming chair made out of genuene leather. It's nearly brand new: I bought it a few months back, and now it just has a few small scratches on the surface, barely noticeable. On top of that, it comes with great back support, adjustable height, very comfortable armrests, and the list goes on.

As you might rememeber, besides studying, I work part-time on weekday evenings, so it would propably be a good idea if you stopped by on the upcoming weekend, which is July, 15th. But keep in mind that I love to sleep in on weekends, so come over anywhere from 12 to 6; I think I'll be up by that time. Anyway, give me a call before coming, just in case.

Take care,
