## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

There are plans to make your local airport bigger and increase the number of flights. You live near the airport. Write a letter to your local council. In your letter

• say where you live
• describe the problem
• say why you do not want the development to take place

Write at least 150 words.

## My answer

Dear Council Representative,

I am writing to draw your attention to the potential negative impact on local communities and me personally due to the planned expansion of the international airport. I currently live in the residential area, which is located on the outskirts of town in the immediate vicinity of the airport: the first terminal is a five minute walk from where I live.

As I live in close proximity to the airport, I am concerned that the planned expansion may exacerbate the pre-existing issues that our neighborhood is grappling with, ranging from the incessant noise from plane take-offs to severe traffic congestion.

Given the importance of the situation, I would like to inquire whether the council would consider alternative solutions, such as building another airport in another remote part of the city, which would be in accordance with safety standards. Should the council proceed with the planned expansion, I might have to resort to taking legal action, since the noise pollution would violate the safe levels, approved by the regulatory institutions.

Sincerely,
