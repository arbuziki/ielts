**Verdict.** Like S5 1B Script D (7), this is an appropriate formal letter with good topic vocabulary. But at 142 words it is under length, and bullets 2 and 3 each rest on a single example, so TA stays at 6. The band matches engnovate's, but this piece only just reaches 7.0.

- **Task Achievement:** Under 150 words, so the house cap of TA 6 applies. The situation is misstated: the council is *considering* closure, but you write "your decision to close". Bullet 2 (one example: pool and playgrounds) and bullet 3 (one effect: health) need more extension.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** One paragraph per bullet, with good reference ("this facility", "The closure"). The "In the first place / Moreover / For instance" chain reads like an essay, and "That being said" is misused before the closing request.
- **Lexical Resource:** Less common items are used appropriately ("sedentary lifestyle", "far-reaching implications", "exacerbate"). On the other side, "largely appreciated" is the wrong collocation and there are 2 spelling errors ("frineds", "helath").
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 5 of 8 sentences are error-free. The participle clause ("Unable to access…") and the non-defining relative are controlled, but tenses drift.

**Key corrections**
- "your decision to close our local sports centre" → "your plan to close our local sports centre" (accuracy: it has not been decided)
- "me and my frineds have been frequently visiting" → "my friends and I have been regularly visiting" (subject pronoun; spelling)
- "people often used to visit the pool, while kids are enjoying the playgrounds" → "people often use the pool, while children enjoy the playgrounds" (consistent tense)
- "return to sedentary lifestyle" → "return to a sedentary lifestyle" (article)
- "largely appreciated" → "greatly appreciated" (collocation)
- "helath-related" → "health-related" (spelling)

**To reach Band 7.5:** Go past 160 words. Add one concrete user group for bullet 2 (e.g. pensioners' aqua classes, school teams) and a second effect for bullet 3 (e.g. teenagers with nowhere to go). End with "I therefore urge you to…" instead of "That being said".
