## Prompt

A friend you made while you were studying abroad has written to ask you for help in finding a job in your country. You have heard about a job in local company that might be suitable for him/her.
Write a letter to this friend. In your letter:
Tell your friend about the job and what sort of work it involves
Say why you think the job would be a suitable for him/her
Explain how to apply for the job

## My answer

Hello John,

How have you been? I've received a letter from you asking about potential positions and I've got some great news for you: I've just learned that a tech company in my town is hiring now. They set off to develop a new traffic monitoring system, which requires a lot of specialists with good technical skills: software developers, DevOps engineers, project managers and so on.

As I remember, your main specialization was cybersecurity. From what I've learned, they are expanding their cybersecurity department, and that's exactly the position that they are interested in. I remember that you were particularly good at finding those backdoors

An acquaintance of mine is friends with a HR manager at the company. If you'd like, you could send me your CV, and I'll pass it over to the manager. It'll spare you some excessive screening and might help you to get a job interview sooner.

Take care,
