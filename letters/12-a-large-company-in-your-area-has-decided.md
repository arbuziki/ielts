## Prompt

A large company in your area has decided to spend a certain amount of money, either to sponsor a local children's sports team for two years, or to pay for two open-air concerts. It has asked for feedback from the general public.
Write a letter to the company. In your letter:
Describe the benefits of sponsoring the sports team
Summarise the benefits of paying for the concerts
Say how you think the company should spend the money

## My answer

Dear Sir,

I am writing to express my profound gratitute for offering to subsidize our local community's social activities. It came to my knowledge that the company has sufficient funds only for one of the following projects.

First, I am aware that you consider supporting our local children's football team for two years. The benefits of this policy would be immeasurable: it would keep children active and immensely bolster their physical development.

Another candidate for subsidizing is two open-air concerts. Sponsoring them would ponentially attract tourists to our town, which would stimulate our local economy. Also, such big event would foster social life and dynamics by giving people an opportunity to unwind.

Having considered both options, I would suggest that you opt for the first one, and sponsor the extracurricular program for our children. Should you proceed with funding the sports team, I firmly believe that the resulting benefits will be immeasurable for the entire community.

Sincerely yours,
