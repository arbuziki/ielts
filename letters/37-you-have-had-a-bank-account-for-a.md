## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have had a bank account for a few years. Recently you received a letter from the bank stating that your account is $240 overdrawn and that you will be charged $70 which will be taken directly from your account. You know that this information is incorrect.

Write a letter to the bank. Explain what has happened and say what you would like them to do about it.

You should write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write your own address. Begin your letter as follows:

Dear Sir,

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing to formally respond to your letter that I received yesterday, claiming that my account is overdrawn and a penalty of $70 is to be deducted from it. I would like to draw your attention to this inaccuracy, as I checked the balance on this account two days ago, and I didn't commit any transaction since then.

The issue might have appeared due to the last purchase I made yesterday, but the monetary amount has been refunded by the seller; thus, I believe, the problem could be related to the desynchronization in your system.

As I believe the data you have provided is inaccurate, I would like to request the full history of transactions committed using the account in question: I would like to look into all expenditures to make sure that the issue is on your side. Should it be confirmed that the root cause is on your side, I would like to inquire whether it would be possible to cancel the penalty.

Sincerely,
