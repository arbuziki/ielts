## Prompt

A museum near your home is looking for people to do part-time voluntary/unpaid work. You would like to do some voluntary / unpaid work at the museum.
Write a letter to the museum director to apply for the voluntary/unpaid work. In your letter
Explain why you want to do voluntary/unpaid work at the museum
Describe some skills and qualities you have that would be useful
Give details of when you would be available for work

## My answer

Dear Director,

I am writing to formally propose my candidature for the voluntary work at your esteemed gallery of modern art. I am a regular visitor of this renowned museum, as I believe I could offer my support for curating exhibitions and organizing events.

It is worth mentioning that I am currently a student of an art school, as I believe my art education and cultural experience will prove to be a valuable asset for the whole museum team. My peers often name my soft skills to be one of my major advantages to fin in your convivial atmosphere.

Should I be granted this opportunity, I will be able to commense my duties this summer upon my graduation, which will take place in two weeks. I am looking forward to join the museum team soon.

Sincerely,
