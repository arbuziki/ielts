## Prompt

WRITING TASK 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You and your family are living in rented accommodation in an English-speaking country. You are not satisfied with the condition of some of the furniture.

Write a letter to the landlord. In your letter
• introduce yourself
• explain what is wrong with the furniture
• say what action you would like the landlord to take

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ................. ,

## My answer

Dear Landlord,

My name is John, I am occupying the apartment on the third floor. I thoroughly enjoy my experience of staying at your place, and I am afraid to disturb you by reporting that I am experiencing an issue with a piece of furniture in my accommodation.

The furniture in question is a chair in my bedroom: it is highly worn and wobbly, with patches of leather are coming off. To be perfectly honest, it is also lacking proper back support and I would like to replace it altogether.

Upon signing the lease agreement, we discussed that I would be provided with a replacement, taking into account the condition of the chair. I completely understand that you have a tight schedule, but it has been a few months already, so I had to order a new chair myself. Therefore, I would like to inquire whether you would consider reimbursing me for this expenditure.

Yours sincerely,
