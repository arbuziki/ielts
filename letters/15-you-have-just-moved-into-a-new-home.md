## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have just moved into a new home and are planning to hold a party. You are worried that the noise may disturb your neighbour.

Write a letter to your neighbour. In your letter
• introduce yourself
• describe your plans for the party
• invite your neighbour to come

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear ............,

## My answer

Dear Neighbor,

My name is John; my fiance and I have just moved into the house, which is just around the corner from where you live. You have a wonderful neighborhood, it's well-served and perfectly maintained. The location is just incredible: there's so much greenery in the vicinity.

We are so excited to announce that we are planning on hosting a small get-together to celebrate our fresh start. We would like to humble ask if you don't mind some loud music and possibly some naked people running around the pool next Sunday. Just kidding, the music won't be very loud! All the jokes aside, I hope we won't cause a lot of disturbance.

By the way, all of our neighbors are invided, which means that we would like to have you over to join our celebration. All the drinks and food are on house! We would be thrilled to share the story of our journey with you!

Best regards,
