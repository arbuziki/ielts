## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You are a student who has returned to your home country. Write a thank-you letter to your homestay family stating what you particularly enjoyed about your stay.

Write at least 150 words.

## My answer

Dear Johnsons,

I just wanted to drop you a line to say that I got back home safely, as I would like to thank you for providing me with this wonderful opportunity of staying at your place: I thoroughly enjoyed the experience and was deeply moved by your generosity and kindness.

When working part-time at local restaurants on weekday evenings, I managed to acquire the unparalleled experinece of interacting with customers in a foreing language. Besides, I will always cherish those moments, as I was playing basketball with your kids in the backyard all evening long. Or a trip to the New York City where I finally got to witness the Statue of Liberty with my own eyes: that was simply breathaking.

I would like to express my sincere gratitude once again, as I believe this journey proves to be one of the most memorable experiences in my lifetime.

Take care,
