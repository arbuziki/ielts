## Prompt

You recently went to a concert and thought one of the singers was very good. You want to tell him how you feel.
Write a letter to the singer. In your letter:
Say how you feel about his performance
Give details of your musical activities
Explain how you would like him to help you with your musical activities

## My answer

Dear CrazyHeadedObliterator,

I'm writing to let you know that your yesterday's gig was just breathtaking: it far exceeded my expectations! Experiencing your band kill the dance floor firsthand was just otherworldly, as my friends and I are still exchanging our shared excitement.

What makes it even more memorable, I've been a huge fan of your band since I was 15. As a matter of fact, you were the reason I started learning to play piano. I was looking up tutorials of your songs on YouTube and practicing finger placement till I managed to master a piece flawlessly. This experience proved highly transformative, since I ended up creating my own small band: we play in local bars mostly, but we enjoy this hobby a lot.

If you happened to be interested, we could play at your concerts to warm up the crowd. We noticed that the band that was tasked with this job yesterday somewhat underdelivered: they were missing the notes and loosing the rythm. Nonetheless, I wish your band all the best and believe that your best bangers are yet to come.

Best regards,
