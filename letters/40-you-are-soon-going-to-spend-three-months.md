## Prompt

You are soon going to spend three months doing work experience in an organisation.
Write a letter to the manager of the organisation where you are going to do work experience. In your letter:
Thank the manager for the opportunity to do work experience
Explain what you hope to learn from the work experience
Ask some questions about the work experience you are going to do

## My answer

Hello Mr Jhonson,

I am sending this letter to you to express my sincere gratitude for providing me with such a unique opportunity to participate in the newest AI training program.

By attending this program, I anticipate that I will be able to grasp the core principles of interacting with large language models. It is rapidly evolving and extremely promising technology in the contemporary world. That being said, I expect that aquiring this useful skill will significantly boost my productivity and improve my performance in the long run.

Additionally, I would like to ask you a few questions regarding the upcoming training program. First, are we going to focus on a specific AI tools or explore the AI basics in general? And second, do you have any feedback from those who have already completed this course?

In conclusion, I would like to thank you again for this offer.

Best regards
