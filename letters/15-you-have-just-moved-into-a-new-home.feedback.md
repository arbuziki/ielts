**Verdict.** The language is close to the S5 1B Script D Band 7 level. Task Achievement is weaker for two reasons: the joke about "naked people running around the pool" is an inappropriate tone for a first letter to a neighbor you are worried about disturbing, and the party plans lack practical detail. The task therefore lands at 7.0, half a band below engnovate, whose TA 8 overlooked the tone lapse.

- **Task Achievement:** All bullets are present. The introduction and invitation are fine, but the plans give only a day and "small get-together", with no time, guest numbers or end time, and noise is the neighbor's main concern. The joke is a minor inconsistency in tone, so TA 6.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Paragraphs follow the bullets. "By the way" downgrades the invitation, which is a main point, into an aside.
- **Lexical Resource:** There are good items ("well-served", "in the vicinity", "have you over"). Against these: "humble ask", "invided", "on house", and the off-register joke.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 6 of 10 sentences are error-free. Errors include a comma splice, a wrong word form ("humble") and article errors ("All the jokes aside", "on house").

**Key corrections**
- "We would like to humble ask if you don't mind" → "We would like to ask whether you would mind" — adverb form and conditional politeness
- "possibly some naked people running around the pool" → (delete) — inappropriate for the reader
- "All the drinks and food are on house!" → "Food and drinks are on the house!" — fixed phrase needs *the*
- "all of our neighbors are invided" → "all our neighbors are invited" — spelling
- "You have a wonderful neighborhood, it's well-served" → "It's a wonderful neighborhood – well served and…" — comma splice

**To reach Band 7.5–8:** Replace the joke with practical reassurance, for example "It will run from 6 to 10 pm, with about 15 guests, and the music will be off by 10." Then make the invitation its own sentence rather than a "by the way".
