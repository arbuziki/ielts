Bullets 1–2 are detailed and the grammar is mostly accurate, but the insurance vocabulary is imprecise and the request is abrupt, so it does not meet Band 8's "appropriately extended" and "precise" standard. The band matches engnovate's, but the profile is reversed: their GRA 6 is too low and their TA and LR 8 are too high.

However, the required opening "Dear Sir or Madam" is ignored, "NYC" and "so expect you to cover" are lapses in tone, and the order of events is confused (the airline told you before you "realized" on arrival).
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Logical paragraphs with good reference ("which I reported…"). The purpose and bullet 1 share a paragraph, and "On top of that" is informal.
- **Lexical Resource:** Good travel vocabulary ("multiple layovers", "connecting flights", "nowhere to be found", "opted for"). The insurance terms are imprecise: "insurance request" (claim), "highly precious" (valuable), "money equivalent" (value), "expenditure" (loss).
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 8 of 9 sentences are error-free. The causative "had to have the company issue" and the past perfect passive are accurate. One sentence carries two errors.

**Key corrections**
- "Dear Manager," → "Dear Sir or Madam," (the prompt specifies this opening)
- "file an insurance request" → "submit a claim under my travel insurance policy" (precise term)
- "highly precious items" → "several valuable items" (collocation)
- "I declared the money equivalent of my belongings as much as $10000, so expect you to cover this expenditure in full." → "I estimate the total value of my belongings at $10,000, and I would be grateful if you could reimburse this amount in full." (missing subject; polite tone; wrong noun)
- "During my last trip to NYC" → "During my recent trip to New York" (register)

**To reach Band 8:** Use insurance terms precisely (claim, policy number, reimburse) and make the request polite. Add one sentence on the evidence you will enclose, such as receipts and the airline's lost-baggage report reference.
