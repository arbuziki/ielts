## Prompt

During a recent plane journey, you sat next to a businessman who owns a chain of restaurants. You talked to him and he suggested that you should contact him about a possible job in one of his restaurants.
Write a letter to this businessman. In your letter:
Remind him when and where you met
Tell him what kind of job you are interested in
Say why you think you would be suitable for the job

## My answer

Dear Mr. Johnson,

I'm writing to follow up our fascinating conversation that we had on our way to New York City. As you might remember, we struck up a conversation while waiting in a TSA line, and later it turned out that we are not only on the same plane, but next to each other, that cannot be a mere coincidence! I remember we were discussing your burgeoning restaurant business throughout the whole flight.

Having said that, I remember that you mentioned that you were having issues with organizing expanding branches and streamlining business operations. Being a manager myself, I gave you some advice on running this business that you fould very insightfull and asked if I could join your team one day. Well, I'm glad to let you know that, as of this moment, I'm on the labor market looking for a new job!

Should I be granted this opportunity to take part in your expanding business, I'm confident that my expertise will become a substantial asset for your company's productivity. I believe that my 10 years of experience in this field will help your company to yield significant profit gains.

Sincerely yours,
