**Verdict.** This is a solid Band 7 letter, comparable to S5 1B Script D. Each bullet has its own paragraph and some development, but the collocations are imprecise and article and tense errors recur. It is a full band below engnovate because LR and GRA 8 need precise word choice and non-systematic errors, and neither holds here.

- **Task Achievement:** All three bullets are covered and highlighted. The suitability bullet rests only on being a native speaker and the degree, with no experience with children. "part-time job position" contradicts the one-year family role. "Frankly speaking" and "On top of that" loosen the formal tone slightly (Band 7).
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphing is logical, one bullet per paragraph. The linkers are stacked ("Besides … On top of that … thus … Frankly speaking"), which is the over-use the CC 7 descriptor mentions.
- **Lexical Resource:** "trimming the bushes", "upkeep and repairs" and "mutually beneficial" are good. "monitoring Australian news", "a vast range of yard work" and "handy when it comes to DIY skills" are imprecise. The closing "Should I be granted this opportunity…" is template text and earns no credit for range.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 3 of 6 sentences are error-free. There are article errors ("Saint Petersburg's University", "that arrangement") and a wrong tense in "used to be".

**Key corrections**
- "the advertisement of which I discovered while monitoring Australian news" → "which I saw in an Australian newspaper" — natural collocation.
- "Saint Petersburg's University" → "St Petersburg University" — no possessive in an institution's name.
- "I used to be particularly handy when it comes to DIY skills" → "I am also good at DIY" — "used to" means you no longer are.
- "I am confident that arrangement will prove" → "this arrangement" — article or determiner.
- "managing house work" → "housework" — one word.

**To reach Band 8:** Extend bullet 1 with a concrete teaching example (tutoring children, a method you would use), and replace stacked linkers with reference ("This experience…").
