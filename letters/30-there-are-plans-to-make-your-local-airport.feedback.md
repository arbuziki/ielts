**Verdict.** The language here is among the cleanest in the batch, and the band is a borderline 7.5. The content is thinner than the S9 Band 9 model, which gives concrete, specific problems: "describe the problem" gets one sentence and the "why not" bullet rests on a legal threat. Engnovate's CC 9 and GRA 9 are too high, because there is noticeable repetition and there are three minor errors.

- **Task Achievement:** The location is well extended (outskirts, a five-minute walk to terminal 1). The problem is only listed ("noise… to severe traffic congestion"), with no example of its effect on daily life. Bullet 3 offers an alternative and a firm but polite warning. That is TA 7: "could be extended more fully".
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Progression is clear, but substitution is weak. "Planned expansion" appears three times, "another airport in another remote part" repeats itself, and paragraph 2 restates the location from paragraph 1.
- **Lexical Resource:** "Exacerbate the pre-existing issues", "grappling with", "incessant noise" and "resort to taking legal action" are precise and appropriate. The slips are "violate the safe levels" (should be "exceed safe limits") and "regulatory institutions" (should be "authorities").
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 3 of 5 long sentences are error-free (60%), below the 70% needed for GRA 8, so GRA 7. The range includes an inverted conditional, relative and participle clauses. The errors are minor and do not form a pattern: "the residential area" (should be "a"), "five minute walk" (needs a hyphen) and a stray comma before "approved".

**Key corrections**
- "I currently live in the residential area, which" → "I live in a residential area that" — article
- "a five minute walk" → "a five-minute walk" — hyphenated compound adjective
- "would violate the safe levels, approved by the regulatory institutions" → "would exceed the safe limits set by the authorities" — collocation and comma
- "building another airport in another remote part of the city" → "building a new airport in a more remote area" — repetition

**To reach Band 8:** Give the problem bullet concrete effects, such as flights over your house every few minutes at night, children unable to sleep, or a 40-minute queue to reach the motorway. Vary the references to "the expansion".
