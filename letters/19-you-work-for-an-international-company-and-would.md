## Prompt

You work for an international company, and would like to spend six months working in its head office in another country.
Write a letter to your manager. In your letter:
Explain why you want to work in the company's head office for six months
Say how your work could be done while you are away
Ask for his/her help in arranging it

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing to formally propose my temporary relocation to the head office in NYC. As we discussed with the senior product manager, since we are aimed to land a new major customer, my immediate presence will substantially reduce communicative overhead due to the current time zone difference. Should I be granted this opportunity, I believe that the resulting expansion of our client base will prove to be a significant asset to our company's success.

Meanwhile, during the time of my absence, I arranged for my colleague to substitue me. Moreover, I discussed with the team whether it would be possible to reschedule some of our online meetings, and I realized that I will be able to attend almost all of them.

Given the expensive nature of the trip in question, I would like to inquire whether the company would consider reimbursing me for the related expenditures.

Yours sincerely,
