**Verdict.** The vocabulary is rich, but on task this falls below the S5 Band 7 anchors. The letter never says what job you want (a manager role has to be inferred) and it gives no date for the flight. Contractions and exclamations also make it too chatty for a business contact. engnovate scores it 0.5 higher because it does not penalise the unanswered second bullet.

- **Task Achievement:** Bullet 2 is not adequately covered because no job title or role is stated, which gives TA 5. Bullet 1 gives the place but not when you met. The tone wavers ("I'm", "Well,", "that cannot be a mere coincidence!") next to formal "Should I be granted".
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The first paragraph is one run-on sentence, and "Having said that" is misused (no contrast follows). Bullets 2 and 3 blur together. Progression is generally clear, but the cohesion is faulty.
- **Lexical Resource:** Some strong items ("struck up a conversation", "burgeoning", "streamlining business operations"). But "on the labor market" is a calque, and "a substantial asset for your company's productivity" is a padded template. 2 spelling errors ("fould", "insightfull").
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 6 of 8 sentences are error-free (75%). The range is wide (inversion, participle clauses, relative clauses, "not only… but"), and the two errors (a missing preposition; a tense shift with a comma splice) do not recur, so GRA 8.

**Key corrections**
- "to follow up our fascinating conversation that we had" → "to follow up on the conversation we had" (preposition; redundancy)
- "it turned out that we are not only on the same plane, but next to each other, that cannot be a mere coincidence!" → "it turned out that we were not only on the same flight but also sitting next to each other." (tense; comma splice; tone)
- "Having said that," → "During the flight," (no contrast is intended)
- "fould very insightfull" → "found very insightful" (spelling)
- "I'm on the labor market looking for a new job!" → "I am currently seeking a new position." (calque; register)
- "a substantial asset for your company's productivity" → "a valuable asset to your company" (collocation)

**To reach Band 7:** State the role outright, e.g. "I am interested in an operations manager position overseeing your new branches", and give the flight date. Remove the contractions and exclamations so the tone stays semi-formal throughout.
