## Prompt

A friend of yours is thinking about applying for the same course that you did at university. He/She has asked for your advice about studying this subject.
Write a letter to your friend. In your letter:
Give details of the course you took at the university
Explain why you recommend the university
Give some advice about how to apply

## My answer

Dear John,

How have you been? I just wanted to drop you a line to tell you about the course, titled Generative AI, that I complited last year. This program is excellent, it provides an exhaustive analysis of large language models, delving deeply into the intricacies of their architecture. You will dissect the multi-head attention mechanism, the tokenization, the back-propagation and other important concepts, which will equip you with incredibly valuable hands-on experience.

I should point out that this program is being taught in just a handful of universities, so don't miss out on this opportunity! Also, the professor who teaches this course is a well-known software developer with over 20 years of experience in the field of machine learning.

I you happen to opt for this course, be aware that they require passing complex exams in order to get enrolled. First, you need to fill out the online form: I will attach the file with the all the instructions. After that, you have to book a date for your exam. You better refresh your basic computer science knowledge, since you'll need it!

Good luck on the exam, I'll be rooting for you!

Take care,
