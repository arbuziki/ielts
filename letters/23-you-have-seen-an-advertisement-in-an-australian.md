## Prompt

You have seen an advertisement in an Australian magazine for someone to live with a family for six months and look after their six-year-old child.
Write a letter to the parents. In your letter:
Explain why you would like the job
Give details of why you would be a suitable person to employ
Say how you would spend your free time while you are in Australia

## My answer

Dear Parents,

I am writing to formally propose my candidature for the position of looking after your six-year-old child that I discovered in a magazine. While child-rearing has always been my strongest skill, I would like to take an opportunity to visit Australia, the place I've always wanted to visit.

I am mindful of the fact that I might apprear too young for this role, since I am only 16, but despite my young age, I have an extensive experience nurturing my younger siblings. As I helped my parents to raise my little brothers and sisters, I managed to develop a number of essential parental skills, such as basic pediatric care, positive reinforcement strategies, healthy dietary planning, and many others.

Should I be granted this opportunity of taking care of your child, I believe this would prove to be mutually beneficial: in my leisure time on weekends, I could venture off exploring the wilderness of this mysterious continent.

Yours sincerely,
