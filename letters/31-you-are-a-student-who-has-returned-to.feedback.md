**Verdict.** This is a borderline 6.5, between the S5 1B Script C Band 6 letter and the S5 1B Script D Band 7 one. The purpose is clear, but the tone shifts, one "enjoyed" point has nothing to do with the family, and sentence control is weak. engnovate's 8s on TA, CC and LR overlook the register drift, the faulty linking and the misspellings.

- **Task Achievement:** All the content is relevant to the purpose, but the restaurant job is not something the homestay family gave you. The tone moves from chatty ("drop you a line", "Take care") to stiff and formal ("express my sincere gratitude"), which the descriptors treat as minor inconsistencies in tone (Band 6). At 151 words it only just meets the minimum.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphs are logical, but the linking is faulty: "as" is used as a general-purpose connector, "Besides" introduces a new memory rather than an extra point, and paragraph 2 ends on a fragment starting with "Or".
- **Lexical Resource:** "cherish" and "deeply moved by your generosity" work well. "acquire the unparalleled experience" is too grand for a family letter. There are 3 spelling errors (experinece, foreing, breathaking), which is "a few" (LR max 7), so LR 7.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 1 of 5 sentences is error-free. The errors are tense ("proves to be"), a fragment, an article ("the New York City") and a misused causal "as".

**Key corrections**
- "as I would like to thank you" → "and to thank you" — "as" does not mean "and".
- "experinece … foreing … breathaking" → "experience … foreign … breathtaking" — spelling.
- "as I was playing basketball with your kids" → "when I played basketball with your kids" — this is a time clause, not a reason.
- "Or a trip to the New York City where" → "And I'll never forget our trip to New York City, where" — fix the fragment and drop the article.
- "this journey proves to be" → "this trip has been" — tense.

**To reach Band 7:** Write the whole letter in a warm, informal voice ("Thanks so much for…", "Love,") and give two or three family-specific memories, each with one detail about what made it special.
