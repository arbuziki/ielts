## Prompt

Your local council is considering closing a sports and leisure centre that it runs, in order to save money.
Write a letter to the local council. In your letter:
Give details of how you and your friends or family use the centre
Explain why the sports and leisure centre is important for the local community
Describe the possible effects on local people if the center closes

## My answer

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am profoundly dissatisfied with your decision to close our local sports centre - this facility has been largely appreciated by our local community for providing affordable, high-quality service.

In the first place, me and my frineds have been frequently visiting the gym situated on the second floor of this centre. Regular workouts helped me to stay fit and significantly improve my overall well-being.

Moreover, this facility has been providing a diverse range of activities for the betterment of our whole community. For instance, people often used to visit the pool, while kids are enjoying the playgrounds.

The closure may have far-reaching implications for our local community. Unable to access this sports centre, people will inevitably return to sedentary lifestyle which, in turn, will exacerbate helath-related problems.

That being said, I hope you will reconsider your decision.

Best regards,
