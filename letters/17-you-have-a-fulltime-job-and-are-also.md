## Prompt

WRITING TASK 1
You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.
You have a full-time job and are also doing a part-time evening course. You now find that you cannot continue the course.
Write a letter to the course tutor. In your letter
• describe the situation
• explain why you cannot continue at this time
• say what action you would like to take
Write at least 150 words.
You do NOT need to write any addresses.
Begin your letter as follows:
Dear ..................................... ,

## My answer

Dear Tutor,

I am writing to express my sincere apology for being no longer able to attend our piano classes in the evening. Having said that, I was thoroughly enjoying the experience: the lessons under your wise supervision helped me to master my finger placement and the arpeggio techinque. The progress we have made together gives me an immense sense of pride and accomplishment.

As things stand, a very important reccuring online meeting at my work has been rescheduled for later, and it happened to overlap with our course.

Having looked into your online calendar with the intent to find an available spot, I discovered the absence of any mutually convinient time slots. Upon consideration, unfortunately, I see no other options to continue with our courses. I would also like to inquire whether it would be possible to refund my remaining membership since I no longer need it.

Yours sincerely,
