**Key corrections**
- "the Intermedia's internal meeting" → "Intermedia's internal meeting" (no article before a possessive name)
- "I might have lost some very important document" → "I may have left a very important document" ("document" is singular countable; "left" fits the situation)
- "loosing such report" → "losing such a report" (spelling; article)
- "That being said," → "As a result," (the linker should signal a consequence, not a contrast)
- "which unfortunately, I did not" → "which, unfortunately, I did not" (commas around the interrupter)
- "the mentioned places" → "both of these places" (natural reference)
