## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

Your neighbours have recently written to you to complain about the noise from your house/flat. Write a letter to your neighbours.

In your letter, you should explain the reasons for the noise, apologise to them and describe what action you will take.

Write at least 150 words.

## My answer

Dear Neighbors,

I am writing to offer my heartfelt apology for the noise disturbance I caused last evening, as I received a formal complaint from my landlord: I was informed that they received multiple complaints from our neighbors on adjacent floors.

This inconvenience was caused by our housewarming gathering party that we hosted in our appartment. As we celebrated, we forgot how late it was; therefore, we forgot to lower the volume of the background music. As the police arrived at the scene, we were detained and taken to the nearest police station, where we had to provide explanation regarding the incident and were given a fine.

Given the severety of this violaton, we have already reached out to the majority of our neighbors and expressed our apologies. Please, rest assured that going forward I will be highly mindful of potential noise levels in order to not cause any undue disturbance in the future.

Sincerely,
