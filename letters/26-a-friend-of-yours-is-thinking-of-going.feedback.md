**Verdict.** The language is at about the S5 1B Script D Band 7 level, but the task fit is weaker. The letter turns a "camping holiday this summer" into a "sole weekend hiking trip", and bullet 1 is generic. Engnovate's LR 8 overlooks three errors in idiomatic phrases.

- **Task Achievement:** Bullet 1 should say why *your friend* would enjoy camping, but the letter gives a generic sentence about wilderness and fresh air. Bullet 2 lists risks and adds useful advice. Bullet 3 refuses with a reason. The friend asked for advice, yet the letter congratulates him on a "decision" and changes the trip type. The details are inaccurate and more extension is needed (TA 6).
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** There is one paragraph per bullet. "Having said that", "If I were you" and "Nevertheless" are used naturally, and the progression is clear.
- **Lexical Resource:** "Tons of useful information" and "rooting for you" suit the informal tone. "Drop you a line" is a stock opener, so it is not counted as range. "Congrat you with", "sole" (should be "solo") and "bugged down" (should be "bogged down") are risk-taking errors. They sit at the top of the 6/7 boundary.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 5 of 7 sentences are error-free, and the second conditional and participle clauses are correct. The preposition errors are "congrat you with" and "the deadline in a project".

**Key corrections**
- "congrat you with a very brave decision" → "say what a great idea it is" — wrong verb form and preposition, and the friend has only asked for advice
- "a sole weekend hiking trip" → "your first camping holiday" — word choice, and it matches the task
- "totally bugged down in my backlog" → "totally bogged down with work" — idiom
- "reaching the deadline in a very important project" → "approaching the deadline on a big project" — preposition
- "set up a camp" → "set up camp" — fixed phrase

**To reach Band 7+ in TA:** Keep to the prompt's scenario. Open bullet 1 with something personal, such as "You've always loved…", and give it a concrete example.
