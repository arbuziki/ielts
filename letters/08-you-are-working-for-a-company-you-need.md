## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You are working for a company. You need to take some time off work and want to ask your manager about this.

Write a letter to your manager. In your letter
• explain why you want to take time off work
• give details of the amount of time you need
• suggest how your work could be covered while you are away

Write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses.

Begin your letter as follows:

Dear .................... ,

## My answer

Hello Dear Manager,

I am writing to formally inquire whether it would be possible for me to take some time off on the next Friday.

I have a dentist appointment, which is scheduled for 2PM. I would not be asking to step away from my desk, but this medical intervention is rather urgent. Unfortunately, I was putting off filling my tooth cavity for a long period of time, and this medical condition has deteriorated, so immediate medical assistance is required.

Having discussed the procedure with a doctor, I was assured that this visit would take no longer than two hours. In conjunction with the travel time, I estimate to be absent for 3 hours in total.

Should I be granted this opportunity to visit the dentist, I could catch up on my work tasks later in the evening, since I work remotely. I also discussed with my coworker whether it is possible to reschedule an online meeting that we were supposed to have during the time of my absence. I was given an affirmative answer without any objections.

Sincerely yours,
