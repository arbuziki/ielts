## Prompt

You have seen an advertisement from a couple, who live in Australia, for someone to teach their two children your language for a year.
Write a letter to the couple. In your letter
Explain why you think you would be suitable for the job
Say what else you could do for the family
Give your reasons for wanting the job

## My answer

Dear Mr. and Mrs. Young,

I am writing to formally express my keen interest in the part-time job position of a Russian language teacher, the advertisement of which I discovered while monitoring Australian news. Being a native speaker, I believe that I will be a strong candidate for this role, as I am currently in my final year of a teaching degree at Saint Petersburg's University.

Besides my teaching skills, I would like to emphasize my strong maintenance skills, as I could help you around the house by handling a vast range of yard work, from trimming the bushes to mowing the lawn. On top of that, I used to be particularly handy when it comes to DIY skills; thus, I can manage various upkeep and repairs.

Frankly speaking, Australia has always been my passion since I was young: I would love to take this opportunity and explore this mysterious continent while helping your family with child-rearing and managing house work. Should I be granted this opportunity, I am confident that arrangement will prove to be mutually beneficial.

Sincerely,
