## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have an English-speaking friend who lives in another country. His son has a short vacation before going to college. Write a letter to your friend. In your letter,
• invite your friend's son to come to your city
• introduce some interesting places to visit
• say something about the weather in the city you live.

Write at least 150 words.

## My answer

Dear John,

How have you been? I just wanted to drop you a line to invite your son over to visit our beloved city of Saint Petersburg, as I heard the boy will be on vacation before applying for college. If I'm not mistaken, the kid used to be a huge fan of Russian classical literature, so, I figured, I would be a great opportunity for him to obtain the hands-on experinece of visiting the motherland of many famous Russian writers.

I'm convinced that your son will be fascinated by the historical heritage and cultural authenticity of Saint Petersburg. It's not a coincidence that the city is called a cultural capital: there are so many places for your son to visit, from the Hermitage to the Peterhof Grand Palace. In my opinion, the Hermitage stands out the most: its rich collection of medieval art is just mesmerising.

Aside from the cultural life, our city is especially pleasant to visit in summer, as the weather is generally mild and fair most of the times. Rains occur sometimes, but they are intermittent and, in my opinion, just emphazie the overall authenticity of this wonderful city.

Take care,
