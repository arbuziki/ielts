## Prompt

You work for an international company. You have seen an advertisement for a training course which will be useful for your job.
Write a letter to your manager. In your letter:
Describe the training course you want to do
Explain what the company could do to help you
Say how the course will be useful for your job

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing you to express my profound interest in the latest online AI training course that I have come across recently while surfing the Internet.

The course in question provides a deep insight into the thechology by delving into the intricacies of large language models. On top of that, its ultimate goal is to familiarize learners with the vast range of modern AI tools.

I would like to ask you to find out if is is possible for the company to provide some financial assistance, since this training program is quite expensive.

AI is at the forefront of technical progress, its immense impact is already seen across many industries. I expect that by getting familiar with this top-notch technology, I could significantly improve my overall performance. Namely, I believe that AI could alleviate a substantial chunk of my routine work - such as summarizing large amounts of information, preparing presentiations, creating reports, just to name a few.

Shall I get this opporunity to partake in this course, the benefits for the company will be indispensable.

Best regards,
