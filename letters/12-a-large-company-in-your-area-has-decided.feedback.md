**Verdict.** This is a clear, well-formatted letter at about the level of the S5 1B Script D Band 7 letter. The bullets are covered but thinly, and the recommendation is backed only by repeating "immeasurable". It is a full band below engnovate because its TA 9 ignores how little development bullets 1 and 3 receive.

- **Task Achievement:** All bullets are clearly highlighted but under-extended (the TA 7 descriptor: "could be more fully extended"). The sports benefits get one sentence. The recommendation gives no reason of its own, and the final sentence is a stock line that restates "immeasurable". The opening thanks the company instead of answering its request for feedback.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphing follows the bullets and progression is clear, but the "First… Another… Also" chain is mechanical and the final sentence repeats the content of paragraph 2.
- **Lexical Resource:** There are good items ("bolster their physical development", "stimulate our local economy"). However, "foster social life and dynamics" is vague, "extracurricular program" misnames a sports team, and there are two misspellings (gratitute, ponentially). The template closing is not counted as range.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 7 of 9 sentences are error-free, with a tense slip and an article slip. The range (inversion, participle clause, relative clause) is decent but relies on stock frames.

**Key corrections**
- "It came to my knowledge" → "It has come to my attention" — fixed expression
- "you consider supporting" → "you are considering supporting" — ongoing action
- "such big event" → "such a big event" — article
- "gratitute / ponentially" → "gratitude / potentially" — spelling
- "the extracurricular program for our children" → "the children's football team" — precision

**To reach Band 8:** Give each benefit a concrete example, such as weekly training or a home tournament. In the recommendation, explain why two years of sponsorship outlast two one-off concerts instead of restating "immeasurable".
