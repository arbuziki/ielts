## Prompt

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You are due to start a new job next week but you will not be able to because you have some problems.

Write a letter to your new employer. In your letter
• explain your situation
• describe your problems
• tell him/her when you think you can start.

You should write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write your own address. Begin your letter as follows:

Dear...............,

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing to express my sincere apology, since I will not be able to stark working at the beginning of the upcoming week: one of my close relatives got injured in a car accident yesterday, which requires my immediate presence. Even though my role is remote, I am affraid, it might still be difficult for me to commence my duties, since the onboarding process requires a lot of effort.

Thankfully, the injury is not a very severe one, but my relatives need me to help them handle all the arrangements and logistics. They are quite elderly, so they would like me to help them with filing an insurance claim.

Given that, I would like to inquire whether it would be possible to postpone my first workday for two weeks. Should I be granted this opportunity, I believe my expertise and technical skills will prove to be a significant asset to our team's productivity.

Sincerely,
