## Prompt

The system used for rubbish/garbage area collection in your local area is not working properly. This is causing problems for you and your neighbours.
Write a letter to the local council. In your letter:
Describe how the rubbish collection system is not working properly
Explain how this is affecting you and your neighbours
Suggest what should be done about the problem

## My answer

Dear Minister,

I am writing to highlight the ongoing issue with new garbage compartment, which has been recently installed near our residential building complex. The problem in question is a set of separate issues: the trash cans are not equipped with lids, the garbage collection occurs rarely, and the area gets blocked by parked cars.

The aforementioned problem adversely affects our community: the wind blows plastic bags out of the trash cans and spreads them out over the whole neighborhood; the intermittent trash collection results in a frequent stench; the parked cars prevent regular garbage collection.

Could you please, at your earliest conveniene, propose a solution regarding this problem? I assume that installing lids, restricting area near the compartment for car parking, and increasing the frequency of garbage collection would prove to be sufficient to solve this issue. I also inquired the propery management company regarding the estimated timeline.

Yours sincerely,
