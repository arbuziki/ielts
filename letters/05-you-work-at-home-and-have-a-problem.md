## Prompt

You work at home and have a problem with a piece of equipment that you use for your job.
Write a letter to the shop or company which supplied the equipment. In your letter:
Describe the problem with the equipment
Explain how this problem is affecting your work
Say what you want the shop or company to do

## My answer

Dear Manager,

I am writing to you to express my profound dissatisfaction with a piece of equipment that I have purchased from your online store.

The device in question is a new model of a high-bandwidth router that I ordered a couple days ago. Upon receiving the delivery, I signed the delivery note since there was no visible damage. After unboxing and plugging the device in, I noticed that it is impossible to hook it up to my computer using the Ethernet cable to perform the initial setup. The device's unresponsiveness indicates that the circuit board must have burned out.

The timing of this issue is immensely unfortunate, given that I use the Internet extensively for work, since I run my own web-server that requires high network throughput. Being unable to process customer requests might result in significant financial loss.

Please, at your earliest convenience, reach out to me to discuss the replacement options. In case of failing to reach an agreement, or if a technical assessment is required, I would have to resort to requesting a full refund in accordance to my two year warranty.

Faithfully yours,
