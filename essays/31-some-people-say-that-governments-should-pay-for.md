## Prompt

Some people say that governments should pay for public health care and education, while others say that it is not governments' responsibility. Please discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## My answer

In the modern era, maintaining an optimal balance between optimizing national bugdets and financing social policies has become a complex challenge. While a significant portion of the population claims that the financing of the social safety net is the direct responsibility of the government, others maintain that acute budgetary constraints will eventually shift this financial burden onto the family unit. While acknowledging the challenges of balancing budgets, I firmly believe that the govenment must play a crucial role in social stewardship.

First and foremost, proponents put forward a compelling argument: the government collects taxes, therefore it must return these funds back to the general public in a form of accessible social services. As balancing a budged poses a major challenge, state authorities should come up with various initiatives to seek additional sources of income, such as progressive taxation, funds reallocation, or the privatization of certain businesses. On top of that, the government should prioritize socail services over less important aspects of the economy, ranging from military expenditures to politicians' compensations.

On the other hand, opponents emphasize the perennial challenges that many countries are grappling with, such as an ageing population and a shrinking workforce. Consequent upon this, allocating sufficient funds for all sectors of the economy constitutes an unfeasible task; therefore, balancing a budget often requires unpopular measures, exemplified by the healthcare system cuts or other reductions.

To conclude, I posit that it is essential to prioritize the social safety net, despite potential challenges arising from redirecting funds from other sectors of the economy: public welfare should be the most important concern for the authorities.
