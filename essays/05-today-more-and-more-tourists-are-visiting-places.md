## Prompt

Today more and more tourists are visiting places where conditions are difficult, such as Sahara Desert or the Antartica.
What are the benefits and disadvantages for tourists who visit such places?

## My answer

In a contemporary world, a burgeoning number of tourists pivot toward rather extreme types of activities. Driven by a constant search for yet another dose of adrenaline, these adventurous individuals are willing to spend a fortune to achieve what they perceive as life-defining milestones: for instance, climbing the summit of the Everest mountain, reaching the North Pole, or partaking in safari rides in the middle of a desolated desert. In this discourse, I will try to examine the merits and demerits of this surging trend for those individuals who have been swept by it.

First and foremost, many people complain that the standard, bland vacation style no longer impresses anyone; people are tired of run-of-the-mill guided tours, lounging by the pool in all-inclusive resorts, or just wandering around cobblestone alleys in the middle of a medieval town. As a matter of fact, some maintain that it is just a natural progression for an experienced tourist. Having tried everything, we are predisposed to seek new exiting types of activities. From this standpoint, these ventures appeal to many of us, since they allow to acquire completely unique experience. On top of that, it is almost a guilty pleasure to show off this adventures to your friends occasionally.

On the other side, the financial aspect of such extreme types of traveling is often claimed to be the major deterrent for the general public. Indeed, only a very select few is able to afford the expenditures related to these luxury vacations. In addition, it is not a coincidence that the insurrace companies sometimes charge double rates, which means that these trips might involve certain risks, including all kinds of injuries, traumas, and other detrimental effects on our well-being.

To conclude, I believe that the advantages largely outweigh disadvantages for those who are well-off enough to be able to afford this travel. For many people such trips can become a lifetime experience that will remain a vivid memory for the rest of their lives.
