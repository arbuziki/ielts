**Verdict.** This is the least templated and most natural essay in the set, at the S10 Band 7 level and close to the S8 7.5 essay. It has a clear position, real examples (con artists, HR interviews) and wide grammatical range. Two misused linkers, several collocation slips and too few error-free sentences keep every criterion at 7, below the S5 2A Band 8.

- **Task Response:** The position (snap judgements are a bad thing) is clear from the start and held throughout. Both body paragraphs are supported with examples. The "merits" you acknowledge are never stated, and the HR example is tangled. That is TR 7.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphing is effective, but "On the other hand" opens a paragraph that *continues* your argument rather than contrasting with it. "Nevertheless" after the conditional is also illogical. That makes CC 7: some inaccuracy.
- **Lexical Resource:** "Benefit of the doubt", "ulterior motives", "disingenuous" and "hit or miss" are used well. There are slips: "just barely touch the surface" (*scratch the surface*), "deeply-seated", "pick up a candidate's capabilities". There are two spelling errors (judment, rerfain).
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** The range is wide (inversions "Not only do…", "Were such qualities…", "Only by… can we", and a cleft), and the errors are non-systematic: parallelism, a doubled preposition, a pronoun shift and "a hit or miss". But only 7 of 11 sentences (64%) are error-free, below the ~70% GRA 8 needs, so GRA 7.

**Key corrections**
- "they just barely touch the surface" → "they only scratch the surface"
- "On the other hand, what snap judgement overlooks" → "Moreover, what snap judgements overlook"
- "attract the wrong person and turn down the right person" → "attracting the wrong person or rejecting the right one"
- "behind the facade they hide their true identity behind" → "behind the facade they present"
- "trying to pick up a candidate's problem-solving capabilities" → "trying to gauge a candidate's problem-solving skills"

**To reach Band 7.5–8:** Proofread the long sentences so that at least 8 of 11 are error-free, which would lift GRA to 8. Check that each linker's logic matches the paragraph it opens, and replace the idioms you are unsure of with the standard collocation.
