## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Some people believe that children should be allowed to stay at home and play until they are six or seven years old. Others believe that it is important for young children to go to school as soon as possible.

What do you think are the advantages of attending school from a young age?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, maintaining an optimal balance between children's autonomy and early age schooling has become a complex challenge. Whether kids should be enrolled in schools at a young age remains a subject of intense debate, as some individuals claim that staying at home until kids are six or seven years old might be more beneficial. While acknowledging some minor concerns, I firmly believe that enrolling kids in schools as early as possible is crucial for them to become well-rounded productive members of society.

First and foremost, many studies have shown that introducing kids to basic skills like reading and writing at a young age can significantly facilitate their educational process in the future, as it allows children to pick up the core academic curriculim with less effort. As a matter of fact, starting their studies at as young as six years old can drastically reduce the complexety of many scientific disciplines, thereby fostering academic excellence in the long-term.

Another important benefit of early studying stems from earlier social interactions, as kids can pick up essential skills, such as collaborative problem-solving, conflict de-escalation, or opposite gender interaction. On top of that, depriving kids from interacting with their peers at a young age creates deeply-seated psychological issues that could potentially adversely imact their adult life.

To conclude, I maintain that enrolling kids in educational institutions is immensely important not only to the family, but to society as a whole, as it yields substantial benefits when it comes to nurturing futher generations.
