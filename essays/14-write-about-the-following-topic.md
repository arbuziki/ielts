## Prompt

WRITING TASK 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Some people prefer to live in a house, while others feel that there are more advantages to living in an apartment.

Are there more advantages than disadvantages of living in a house compared with living in an apartment?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, maintaining an optimal balance between the ecological benefits of suburbia and the numerous work opportunities of urban city remains a complex challenge. While a burgeoning segment of the population escapes polluted metropolises in favor of the serenity of private houses, located mostly on the outskirts of town, many others claim that urban advantages ultimately outweigh.

First and foremost, the trend of moving to suburbs is largely attributed to many factors ranging from better ecological conditions to the overall better quality of life. Owning a private house is often perceived as an ultimate life success and is desired by many; it's a perfect environment for bringing up kids. The most important benefit in my opinion stems from its ecological benefits. Indeed, many studies have shown that chronic exposure to high levels of polluted air results in reduced life expectancy for those living in industrial parts of the city. On top of that, the incessant noise, which many city residents are subjected to on a daily basis, takes a psychological toll on their mental well-being.

But on the other hand, I must acknowledge many infamous disadvantages of private houses when compared to apartments such as: an extended commute time from the edge of town, lack of many essential amenities, and the overall remoteness from any social activities. This is exactly where an apartment, located in the center of a bustling city, stands out: educational institutions, public services, cultural facilities, everything is in close proximity. Moreover, getting around the city is often more convenient due to various public transportation options.

To summarize, I don't have a firm stance on this matter. Both types of accomodation offer the equal set of the merits and the demerits that in total even out each other, in my opinion. Taking everything into account, I think that this choice is a matter of personal preference and one's particular life circumstances, so that no general advice can be given.
