**Verdict.** This is below the S8 7.5 and S10 7 essays. Like S5 2B Band 6, one view is covered much more fully than the other, and the final opinion contradicts itself. engnovate's TR 8 ignores both problems.

- **Task Response:** The "relevant and useful" view is never argued. It is dismissed as "idle chitchat", so the coverage is lopsided. The conclusion says the information is "not only relevant", which reverses your actual position, and the "work and life balance" remark is off-topic. This is the TR 6 feature "conclusions may be unclear".
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The four paragraphs progress logically, and "In turn" and "Hence" are used well. Body 1 is only two sentences long, and the opening frame of the conclusion is a template.
- **Lexical Resource:** Some good items ("susceptible to biased judgement", "glass ceiling", "pad out an interview"). There are 3 spelling errors (discource, pontential, do→due) and one wrong collocation ("exercise an overreach in").
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 7 of 13 sentences are error-free. The errors cluster in complex sentences: verb patterns ("risks to lose") and agreement ("this kind of conversations").

**Key corrections**
- "is not only relevant, but often is used" → "is not only irrelevant but is often used" — the wrong word reverses your opinion
- "risks to lose a worker do to a pontential maternity leave" → "risks losing a worker due to potential maternity leave" — verb pattern and spelling
- "this kind of conversations is nothing more but just an idle chitchat" → "this kind of conversation is nothing more than idle chitchat"
- "this asking personal questions" → "asking personal questions"
- "exercise an overreach in one's private life" → "intrude into candidates' private lives"

**To reach Band 7:** Give the "useful" view a real argument, for example that hobbies show teamwork or how well a person fits the culture, before you rebut it. Then state the same position in the introduction and the conclusion.
