## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Being a celebrity - such as a famous film star or sports personality - brings problems as well as benefits.

Do you think that being a celebrity brings more benefits or more problems?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern digital era, it is becoming increasingly easy to become famous due to the prevalence of social media, streaming services, and online platforms. While fame brings substantial financial benefits, at the same time, it comes at a cost of mental well-being for some. Whether the merits of becoming a celebrity offset the demerits remains a subject of intense debate. Reflecting on this discourse, while acknowledging the drawbacks of being a celebrity, I lean toward the opinion that the advantages massively outweigh.

First and foremost, it is undeniable that fame comes with plenty of benefits ranging from immense wealth to access to privileged social circles. This appeals to large audiences, especially during the contemporary times when the overwhelming majority of people live paycheck to paycheck. On top of that, becoming a celebrity often guarantees a lifelong financial security. For instance, having retired, many musicians, actors, or writers continue to benefit from their intellectual property via royalties or by licensing the author rights. Last but not least, the immense influence of famous people could be utilized for the betterment of all people by promoting charity, healthy life style, and other social initiatives.

On the other hand, opponents put forward irrefutable shortcomings. Being constantly under the surveillance of paparazzi takes a psychological toll on mental well-being. Apart from that, a very negative trend began to surge, the phenomenon known as "clout chasing": in the pursuit of fame, teenagers started to harass regular people while filming it with the intent of posting it on social media and going viral.

To conclude, I am firmly convinced that the set of advantages massively surpasses the negatives, of which there are not many.
