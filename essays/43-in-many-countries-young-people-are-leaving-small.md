## Prompt

In many countries, young people are leaving small towns and villages to live in large cities.
What problems does this cause for the places they leave behind? What can be done to solve these problems?

## My answer

The recent trend of young people leaving small towns and villages has become a negative development that many nations are grappling with. This essay argues that such a trend adversely impacts rural areas and the local communities, resulting in their slow decline. Solving this issue requires diligent effort and legislative intervention from the government: it must provide sufficient stimulus for young people to stay in their own hometowns.

The phenomenon in question has many repercussions on small towns. First, the departure of young specialists strips small towns of the workforce, essential to keep local businesses operational. Admittedly, small businesses rarely can offer competetive income to entice young employees to stay, thereby creating a self-perpetuating loop where both workers and business owners eventually leave. Second, the trend undermines small communities: the population is ageing rapidly, placing an immense strain on local budgets and forcing governments to reallocate substantial funds to subsidise retirees.

Being one of the major threats that small towns are facing, the relocation must be addressed by authorities, as they should put forward comprehensive policies to incentivise young individuals to settle down in their hometowns. As the trend stems primarily from insufficient job opportunities, the government must initiate various programs, ranging from tax cuts to subsidies for major corporations to incentivise them to establish local branches and manufacturing facilities. Limited education options is another root cause driving young people to leave, and the effective strategy to counteract it is facilitating the return of young specialists to their hometowns upon graduation by introducing affordable housing initiatives.

To conclude, the migration of young individuals to big cities poses a major threat to small towns and villages. I firmly believe that it is the primary responsibility of the government to step in and reverse this trend using a wide range of legislative initiatives, from attracting major businesses to incentivising young people to return to their hometowns.
