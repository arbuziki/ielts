**Verdict.** This is below the S5 2B Band 6 script. The "why" question is misread: you explain poor *disposal* in developing countries, not why we *produce* more garbage. That falls under the TR 5 limiter, "main parts incompletely addressed". engnovate's CC 8 and LR 8 overlook the confused paragraph topic and 8 misspellings.

- **Task Response:** Paragraph 2 never names a cause of increased waste, such as packaging, fast fashion or disposable goods. The solutions paragraph is relevant but over-general ("prohibiting the usage of plastics"), and it moves the responsibility to international organizations instead of governments.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** "First and foremost, the implications ... have already manifested" opens a paragraph that should be about causes, so its central topic is unclear (CC 6). The conclusion repeats "threat" and adds alarm but no new synthesis.
- **Lexical Resource:** 8 spelling errors (irreversable, immese, centrury, govenment ×3, unfortunatelly, intoducing) and inflated or illogical phrases ("most unprecedented threats", "perilous hazards", "ecologically clear world"). The errors do not impede meaning, but they are more than "a few", so LR 6.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 8 of 11 sentences are error-free. The errors are a tense clash ("had become ... have concluded") and a missing article.

**Key corrections**
- "the exact root cause: the improper disposal of garbage" → "one root cause: the rise of single-use packaging": answer the question that was asked
- "As the adverse effects of this issue had become more prevalent, many studies have undoubtedly concluded" → "As the effects have become more visible, studies have concluded": tense consistency
- "one of the most unprecedented threats" → "an unprecedented threat": "unprecedented" cannot be graded
- "ecologically clear world" → "ecologically clean world": wrong word
- "the perilous hazards of this issue" → "the consequences": tautology

**To reach Band 6.5–7:** Plan both questions before writing. Give two real causes of *more* garbage, such as consumerism or packaging, each with an example, and then two government measures that match those causes.
