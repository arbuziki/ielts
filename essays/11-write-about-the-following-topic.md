## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Some people think that it is better to educate boys and girls in separate schools. Others, however, believe that boys and girls benefit more from attending mixed schools.

Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, the notion of "gender-segregated education" is rapidly becoming an anachronism. While the overwhelming majority of nations have eliminated this, what some pople call, archaic approach, some individuals advocate for the reinstating of separated education, pointing out certain benefits, such as better retention and academic performance. In this discourse, I will examine the merits and the demerits of this phenomenon, and I will provide my own firm stance on this matter.

First and foremost, proponents of integrated education for boys and girls put forward a compelling argument: humanity has come a long way fighting any forms of segregation, whether it is based on race, gender, religion, and so on. The abundance of progressive movements and human right advocates resulted in a burgeoning segment of the population strongly rejecting any forms of separation. On top of that, many studies have shown that it is vital for boys and girls to grow together in order to form fully-fledged, socially fitting adult individuals. Stripping children of social interactions with the opposite gender in young age might have lifelong impact on their psychological development.

On the other hand, some people appeal to the main purpose of educational establishments: providing quality education. Indeed, isolating youth from the opposite gender fosters academic performance, since teenagers focus on studying instead of social interactions. Furthermore, opponents point out that parents should enroll their children in extracurricular classes for social interactions, while school is strictly for studying.

In my opinion, while acknowledging that gender-isolated education might yield some minor benefits, I maintain that the adverse effects massively outweigh the advantages. When it comes to me, I assume that I would have had severe communication issues and insecurities, if I had been isolated from interacting with the opposite sex.
