## Prompt

WRITING TASK 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Today more people are travelling than ever before.

Why is this the case?

What are the benefits of travelling for the traveller?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, the notion of "traveling is for the rich" is rapidly becoming an anachronism: a large segment of the international population ventures abroad to explore new places and experience new cultures firsthand. In this essay I will examine this phenomenon and provide an exhaustive analysis as to the reasons behind this burgeoning trend and will shed light on the benefits that accompany traveling.

First and foremost, this trend is largely attributed to a couple of main factors. First, people nowdays deprioritize the traditional value of starting family early, and shift focus toward a self-centered lifestyle, which includes traveling as an essential aspect of self-development. Second, traveling is becoming increasingly affordable to wider audiences as the result of prevalent enonomic prosperity observed worldwide. Indeed, the abundance of low-cost carriers and reasonable priced lodging conveniently accessible via online services like AirBnB results in surging numbers of international tourists.

When it comes to the benefits of traveling, I maintain that they are incredibly diverse and numerous. To begin with, immersing ourselves into local cultures and expanding our social circles allows us to immensely saturate our lives with impactful events. Reflecting on my personal experience, I managed to meet plenty of fascinating people all around the world, some of whom have become my lifelong friends. Whether it is wandering through the cobblestone alleys in the heart of a medieval city, or climbing the summit of some famous mountain, all those experiences are remarkably meaningful. Indeed, never leaving our home country makes us extremely short-sighted and insular, while traveling allows us to drastically broaden our horizons.

To conclude, I am elated to see that many people nowdays have an opportunity to explore other countries, make new international friends, and saturate their lives with meaningful experiences.
