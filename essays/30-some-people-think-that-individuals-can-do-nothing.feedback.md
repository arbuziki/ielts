- **Task Response:** The position in the intro and conclusion is clear (individuals matter), both sides are covered, and the activism point is extended with examples (dumping waste, municipal garbage). Body 2 calls the opposing case "irrefutable arguments" and never rebuts it, and individual action is reduced to activists rather than everyday choices such as transport or consumption. That is TR 7 with over-generalized, partly unfocused support, as in Essay 09, which also left "irrefutable" objections unanswered.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Paragraphing is logical and the progression clear. Body 2 does not link back to the thesis, and "First and foremost… On top of that… On the other hand… Furthermore" is a mechanical chain.
- **Lexical Resource:** "fierce dissent" and "notorious environmental violations" are effective. Against them: five spelling errors ("reponsibility", "aqute", "recenthly", "coprorations", and "stir" for *steer*), which is "some" errors (LR 6), plus "afflicted" for *inflicted*, a wrong word that distorts meaning.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 5 of 8 sentences are error-free and complex forms are well used. There are basic countability and preposition errors ("many research", "future generation", "disposing municipal garbage").

**Key corrections**
- "most of the impact is being afflicted by industrial companies" → "most of the damage is being inflicted by industrial companies" (wrong word)
- "already stir the trajectory" → "already steer the trajectory" (spelling: steer)
- "many research has shown" → "much research has shown" (countability)
- "skeptics put forward irrefutable arguments" → "skeptics put forward strong arguments" (word choice that concedes the debate)
- "improperly disposing municipal garbage" → "improperly disposing of municipal waste" (dependent preposition)
- "the adverse effects to the environment caused by large coprorations" → "the adverse effects on the environment caused by large corporations" (preposition, spelling)

**To secure Band 7:** End body 2 with a rebuttal, for example: emissions persist, but consumer demand for EVs pushed carmakers to change. Proofread the spelling of key words.
