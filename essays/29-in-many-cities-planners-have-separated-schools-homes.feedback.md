**Verdict.** At the S10 Band 7 level: the argument is sound, but some expression is clumsy or imprecise. It does not match S8 7.5, where structures are varied and claims are hedged. Here nearly every sentence follows the same "…, which… thereby…" pattern, so GRA 8 (engnovate) is not supported despite reasonable accuracy.

- **Task Response:** The position is clear (disadvantages outweigh) and both sides are weighed. The causal chain is well extended: separation → car commuting → inactivity → strain on the healthcare system. However, the intro premise is confused, since zoning tends to *cause* sprawl rather than counteract it. The second advantage ("planning the itinerary") is thin, and nothing is exemplified.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Logical paragraphs. Repetition weakens the flow: "minor" appears three times in the last two paragraphs, and "easier… much easier" sits in one sentence.
- **Lexical Resource:** "exacerbate traffic congestion" and "commute between those districts" are apt. One spelling error ("thier"). "segregation" has unwanted connotations, "distinctive zones" is a word-form slip, and "school areas, or educational institution areas" says the same thing twice.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 6 of 8 sentences are error-free. The range is narrow (long chained clauses), and article slips recur ("such an artificial segregation", "lack of physical activity").

**Key corrections**
- "voice thier concerns" → "voice their concerns" (spelling)
- "such as residential areas, school areas, or educational institution areas" → "such as residential, educational and commercial zones" (redundant list)
- "designating distinctive zones" → "designating distinct zones" (word choice)
- "such an artificial segregation" → "such artificial separation" (uncountable noun, connotation)
- "will result in lack of physical activity" → "will result in a lack of physical activity" (article)
- "As some developers attempt" → "As some planners attempt" (the prompt's agent)

**To reach Band 8:** Ground one disadvantage in a concrete case, such as a suburb where every school drop-off needs a car. Vary sentence structure with some shorter sentences, a conditional or a fronted clause, instead of chaining "which… thereby".
