## Prompt

Nowadays many people complain about not getting enough sleep.
What are problems caused by lack of sleep?
What can be done about this lack of sleep?

## My answer

In the modern era, trying to keep up with the relentless pace of modern life, many individuals often find themselves suffering from sleep deprivation and the resulting adverse effects on both mental and physical well-being. As many nations are grappling with the repercussions of such a negative trend, there is a burgeoning segment of government representatives that draws attention to this complex challenge and calls for immediate actions. In this discourse, I will outline the demerits of sleep deprivation and will provide potential actions that can be taken in order to counteract the negative impact of this phenomenon.

First and foremost, many studies have decisively concluded that the inability for quality sleep is extremely detrimental for one's physical health: the accumulated stress can ultimately result in insomnia, creating a self-perpetuating loop that is likely to eventually spirale out of control. Aside from health-related problems, the resulting incapacity for prolonged, sustained work poses a serious threat for a whole nation, in fact, being one of the major reasons that undermines economic growth across many countries.

Given the severity of the situation, it is vital for the government to step in and take decisive actions in order to clamp down on this trend, thereby eliminating the root causes of this phenomenon: the overwhelming amounts of anxiety, arising mostly from stressful workplace, and the ubiquity of social media. Consequently, state authorities must put foward various initiatives to improve workplace environments, ranging from prolonged vacation periods to the prohibition of regular overworks. Aside from that, the govenment should carry out social campaings with the intent to make people aware of the potential risks of the excessive use of online platforms.

To conclude, sleep deprivation has surfaced as one of the most formidable challenges confronting humanity in the contemporary times. As this problem places an immense strain on modern societies, governments must put forward comprehensive policies to counteract the imact of this trend.
