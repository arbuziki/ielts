WRITING TASK 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Some people feel that entertainers (e.g. film stars, pop musicians or sports stars) are paid too much money.

Do you agree or disagree?

Which other types of job should be highly paid?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, the vast chasm between celebrities and essential workers is rapidly widening: while a large segment of the population lives paycheck to paycheck, celebrities are enjoying a luxurious lifestyle. Despite some minor financial benefits for local budgets, I posit that the income discrepancy between the haves and the have nots, being observed in a contemporary world, is manifestly inequitable and ultimatelly is to the detriment of the whole nation.

First and foremost, proponents of capitalistic, profit-centric model put forward arguments, which I find to be unconvincing: the income of celebrities is a reflection of their worth on the market. On top of that, entertainers' tax contributions help bolster economic growth. While acknowledging the principles of capitalistic economies, I emphasize that the vastness of the pay gap is exactly what makes this discrepancy a systemic issue. Indeed, a thousandfold discrepancy between famous people and essential workers bears little resemblance to the vibrant society that very nation is striving for.

In my opinion, the government must prioritize reducing the gap by introducing various initiatives: improving safety net for regular employees, increasing taxation for the rich, phasing in progressive taxes, to name a few. While famous people essentially produce nothing of value, it is regular workers that keep the lights on, and they often end up being left behind. The govenment should redirect its focus toward supporting such important professions as: school teachers, doctors, plumbers, truck drivers, electrics, and many more.

To conclude, I believe that income inequality is one of the most challenging systemic issues that many modern nations are grappling with. My firm stance is that this issue is exaclty where the government must step in: some degree of legislative intervention is required to clamp down on the growing income discrepancy between immensely rich celebrities and essential workers, who are struggling to stay afloat.
