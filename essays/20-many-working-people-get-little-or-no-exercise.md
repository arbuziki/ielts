## Prompt

Many working people get little or no exercise either during the working day or in their free time, and have health problems as a result.
Why do many working people not get enough exercise?
What can be done about this problem?

## My answer

In the modern era, the famous joke about parents walking miles to go to school on a daily basis is rapidly becoming an anachronism: the burgeoning crisis of sedentary lifestyle has become a new reality that many nations are grappling with. While many studies have shown that such lifestyle proves to cause adverse health-related effects, governments are struggling to clamp down on this trend, which results in reduced life expectancy, thereby exacerbating economic deprivation. In this essay, I will provide an exhaustive analysis regarding the root cause of this phenomenon, while suggesting potential solutions.

First and foremost, this trend is largely attributed to the technical advancements of the modern era, which drastically reduced the necessity for physical effort. The ubiquity of cars, elevators, escalators, and other types of transportation, alleviated the physical exertion, but inadvertently consigned individuals to a stationary lifestyle. Second, it is becoming increasingly difficult to keep up with the relentless pace of the contemporary world: daily commutes, traffic jams, and errands consume the most of our time outside working hours, leaving little to no spare time for fitness.

In order to counteract this issue, I maintain that the government must step in. In fact, by introducing various initiatives, such as: subsidizing gym memberships, promoting healthy lifestyles, building outdoor free gyms, and others, this trend can be largely reversed. On top of that, as scientists believe cars to be among the largest contributing factors, the govenment can increase taxation on car ownership, while offering financial support for healthy types of transportation, like bicycles.

To conclude, I firmly believe that the trend in question is one of the most notorious problems, burdening entire nations. As people struggle to set aside sufficient time for workouts, govenmests must foster healthy lifestyles by providing diverse stimuli for individuals to exercise.
