## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

As part of a class assigment you have to write about the following topic:

We are becoming increasingly dependent on computers. They are used in business, hospitals, crime detection and even to fly planes. What things will they be used in the future? Is this dependence on computers a good thing or should we be more suspicious of their benefits?

You should write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern digital era, a burgeoning segment of the employees have become significantly reliant on computers: many institutions and businesses have successfully adopted this advanced technology. While the trend has proven to significantly boost people's productivity, skeptics draw attention to the potential risks of the overreliance on these electronic devices. In this discourse, I will outline other potential areas where this technology can be applied and provide my opinion on whether the merits outweigh the risks.

First and foremost, computers have already been firmly established as indispensable tools in a wide range of various fields, such as the healthcare, law enforcement, aviation, to name a few; thus, it is abundantly clear that the trend will gain momentum in the future, spilling over into new sectors of the economy. First, the government could utilize computers to introduce better legislative initiatives by analysing large amounts of data from vast sources of information. Second, environmental protectionists could use benefit from using computers, as they can help with gathering and analysing weather-related data.

Reflecting on this phenomenon, opponents have raised concerns regarding whether this shift carries more benefits to the general public, claiming that such a dependency might consign workers to a state of profound incapacity to perform their duties without using computers. As a matter of fact, some studies have shown that the overuse of computers can gradually reduce people's cognitive abilities: some individials can no longer perform even simpliest arithmetic operations without using a calculator. The recent advancements in the Artificial Intelligence field even further exacerbates the problem, as it gradually takes over congnitive-intensive, abstract work.

To conclude, I maintain that the use of computers will expand even further. As I acknowledge the merits of this digitalization, I would emphasize that the overuse of electronic devices can be detrimental for our cognitive abilities in the long term.
