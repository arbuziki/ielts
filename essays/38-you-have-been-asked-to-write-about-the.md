## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

You have been asked to write about the following topic.

Some people believe that children's leisure activities must be educational, otherwise they are a complete waste of time.

Do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your experience.

You should write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, a burgeoning segment of the population enrolls their kids in diverse extracurricular activities. Whether those programs should be primarily education-oriented remains a subject of intense debate, as skeptiks claim that otherwise such activities provide little to no value. While acknowledging the merits of studying-focused activities, I posit that the variety of such programs is essential in order for kids to become well-rounded, productive memebers of society.

First and foremost, many studies have shown that sport-related activities, such as swimming, football, or running, are vital for for both physical and mental development especially at a young age; thus, it is counterintuitive for parents to focus strictly on educational extracurricular programs. Instead, by diluting the core academic curriculum with sport games and exercises, kids are able to unwind and socialize, thereby fostering the main educational process.

Moreover, the total amount of workload that teenagers have to endure during their main curriculum proves to be overwhelming for many kids: many students complain that these pressures reduce cognitive capacity, which creates a self-perpetuating loop of stress that is very difficult to clamp down on. In light of the above, additional after-school studies-unrelated activities can help counteract those stresses, as they provide so-called "context switching" that acts as a massive stress-relief. By partaking in fun events, command games, or other social events, kids can interact with peers, which is immensely important for their overall mental well-being.

To conclude, despite the benefits of educational after-school programs, I firmly believe that it is crucial to combine the standard educational process with various additional fun classes, thereby alleviating pressures from studies, while fostering physical and social development.
