## Prompt

WRITING TASK 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Some people think it would be a good idea for schools to teach every young person how to be a good parent.

Do you agree or disagree with this opinion? Describe the skills a person needs to be a good parent.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, maintaining an optimal balance between teaching scientific disciplines and parental skills remains a challenge. While a burgeoning segment of the population advocates that schools must partake in raising kids by introducing them to the basics of parenting, others claim that the main goal of any educational institution is to teach science. Despite some minor drawbacks, I posit that divese parental programs are vital for children to become well-rounded fitting members of our society.

First and foremost, I maintain that it is getting increasingly difficult for parents to keep up with the relentless pace of modern life, which might result in them inadvertently cutting corners on raising children. Thus, schools could step in to fill this gap by phasing in various subjects ranging from managing the family budget, to practical skills such as changing diapers. Such school environments could put across the importance of being a parent in order to foster next generations of vibrant societies. Last but not least, these programs might help the government to clamp down on juvenile delinquency that many nations are grappling with.

On the other hand, I would like to emphasize the voluntary nature of such programs: parents should be able to opt out at any time if they desire so, because I believe in the sanctity of parenting. I must also underscore that additional parenting initiatives must not come at the cost of reduced quality of regular subjects, since they remain a priority.

To conclude, while acknowledging that parents must bear the main responsibility of raising kids, I firmly believe that schools should facilitate children's upbringing by sheding light on many important aspects of their future adult life, including the essential skill of parenting.
