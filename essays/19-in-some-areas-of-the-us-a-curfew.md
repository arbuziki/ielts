## Prompt

In some areas of the US, a 'curfew' is imposed, in which teenagers are not allowed to be out of doors after a particular time at night unless they are accompanied by an adult.
What is your opinion about this?

## My answer

In the modern era, maintaining an optimal balance between childrens' autonomy and preventing juvenile delinquency has become a complex challenge, forcing a trade-off between restrictive supervision and crime safety. In order to clamp down on surging crime rates, the US government imposed a curfew on minors; whether this action is justified remains a subject of intense debate. While acknowledging the potential demerits of this initiative, I firmly believe that the resulting reduction in crime rates will be significant.

First and foremost, proponents of this initiative emphasize the skyroketing rates of crimes commited by minors, ranging from burglary to car theft: the trend, many nations are grappling with, hit the US the most. This phenomenon is largely attributed to the deteriorating situation in the economy: many households live paycheck to paycheck, forcing both parents to work overtime, which drastically reduces capacity for proper parenting. Consequently, the youth ends up on the street, frequently joining gangs that wreak havoc on our societies. Given the severity of the situation, the curfew is needed to alleviate the pressure on the law enforcement authorities. On top of that, keeping teenagers off the street during the nighttime is for their own safety.

Conversely, it is abundantly clear that such initiative constitutes an overreach into personal liberties. The approach basically lumps all children together, imposing severe restrictions on everybody because of crimes, commited by a few "bad apples." Having said that, the mitigating factor is that children are allowed to go outside at night, when accompanied by an adult.

To conclude, I am convinced that this temporary decision is justified. Once the peak of the crisis is over, the restrictions will be lifted and things will go back to normal.
