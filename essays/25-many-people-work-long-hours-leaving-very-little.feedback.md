- **Lexical Resource:** Some good idiomatic items: "takes a psychological toll", "spiraling out of control", "financial cushion". Against them: two spelling or word-form errors ("consitutes", "gravite") and "era of economic deprivation" used imprecisely.

**Key corrections**
- "consitutes a necessity" → "constitutes a necessity" (spelling)
- "many people gravite toward" → "many people gravitate toward" (verb form)
- "The adverse effects… completely eclipses the minor merits" → "…completely eclipse the minor merits" (subject–verb agreement)
- "while wages remained stagnant, the decision of spending extra hours" → "while wages have remained stagnant, the decision to spend extra hours" (tense, verb pattern)
- "thereby securing stable future" → "thereby securing a stable future" (article)
- "As financial benefits might be alluring to some, even they are not guaranteed" → "Although financial benefits may be alluring, even they are not guaranteed" (concession)

**To reach Band 8:** Replace "many studies" with one concrete mechanism or example, such as burnout leading to sick leave and lost income. Add a sentence explaining why the financial necessity argument is still outweighed.
