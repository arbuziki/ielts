## Prompt

In the future, more people will choose to go on holiday in their own country and not travel abroad on holiday.
Do you agree or disagree?

## My answer

In the modern era, a burgeoning segment of the population ventures abroad to explore other countries and immerse itself into foreign cultures. While traveling to foreign nations has become largely affordable for wide audiences, whether it will stay the same in the future remains a subject of intense debate, as opponents predict that people will eventually fall back to domestic vacations. While acknowledging the merits of domestic tourism, I maintain that traveling abroad will remain the preferrable option for the overwhelming majority of people.

First and foremost, proponents highlight the scarcity of choice when considering merely the local relaxation sector, from geographical limitations to the cultural inferiority. In fact, due to unfavorable geographical placement, many countries simply are not able to provide recreational beach-resort services, which many tourists aspire to. Additionaly, many individuals are interested in exploring historical landmarks, medieval architecture, and other cultural hotspots that might not be prevalent in one's home country. In my opinion, the unprecedented variety of choice is the most decisive factor in favor of international traveling.

Conversely, some skeptics emphasize the rising costs of the logistics of international traveling, ranging from plane tickets to hotel reservations. Broadly speaking, that is a compelling argument; however, reflecting on my personal experience, many local accommodations often overcharge for similar or even lower level of service, when compared to foreign counterparts. On top of that, the COVID lockdown has proven that in the absence of competition from international players, the relaxation sector blatantly abused their monopoly status and nearly doubled the prices.

To conclude, I firmly believe that international tourism will remain the go-to option for many, as it offers unparalleled variety of choice and affordability.
