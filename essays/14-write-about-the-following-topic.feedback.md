- **Task Response:** You discuss both sides, but the introduction gives no answer, and the conclusion contradicts itself ("I don't have a firm stance" vs "they even out" vs "personal preference"). This is TR 6: "conclusions may be unclear". Paragraph 2 also mixes house ownership with suburban pollution.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Progression is clear, and "This is exactly where an apartment ... stands out" is effective substitution. The introduction ends mid-idea ("ultimately outweigh."), and "But on the other hand" is too informal for a paragraph opening.
- **Lexical Resource:** Some good collocations ("chronic exposure", "takes a psychological toll"), but there are several imprecise ones: "urban city" (tautology), "infamous disadvantages", "even out each other". One spelling error (accomodation).

**Key corrections**
- "many others claim that urban advantages ultimately outweigh." → "...that the advantages of urban living outweigh these benefits.": verb needs an object
- "the numerous work opportunities of urban city" → "the job opportunities of the city": tautology and article
- "many infamous disadvantages" → "several well-known disadvantages": "infamous" means notorious for something bad
- "offer the equal set of the merits and the demerits that in total even out each other" → "offer comparable advantages and disadvantages that balance each other out": articles and word order
- "so that no general advice can be given" → "so no general advice can be given": "so that" expresses purpose

**To reach Band 7.5:** State "the advantages roughly balance out" in the introduction and argue towards it. Make the conclusion one consistent sentence.
