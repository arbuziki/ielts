## Prompt

Some people think that individuals can do nothing to improve the environment, and only the government and big companies can make a difference. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## My answer

In the modern era, the irreversible environmental damage caused by human activity has surfaced as one of the most formidable challenges confronting humanity. As most of the impact is being afflicted by industrial companies and manufacturing enterprises, whether individuals can play a significant role in reversing this trend remains a subject of intense debate. While acknowledging that the burden of this reponsibility is shouldered mostly by large corporations, I firmly believe that people can play a crucial role in counteracting this trend.

First and foremost, many research has shown that environmental activists already stir the trajectory of this negative trend in the right direction: by expressing their fierce dissent, these people push state authorities to take legislative action against the most notorious environmental violations, such as dumping waste into rivers, or improperly disposing municipal garbage. On top of that, environmental advocates raise an aqute awareness of the importance of preserving our planet for future generation, thereby educating larger audiences to shoulder the burden of environmental protection.

On the other hand, skeptics put forward irrefutable arguments: the largest source of environmental pollution, the emissions from internal combustion engines, will linger; thus, any small actions taken by individuals will be negligible on the larger scale. Furthermore, the extraction of fossil fuel has been ramping up recenthly, as the global stagnation across many nations forces governments to utilize traditional sources of energy, instead of transitioning to renewable, clean technologies.

To conclude, I maintain that the impact of individuals is significant and extremely important, as by keeping constant pressure on the authorities people can help counteract the adverse effects to the environment caused by large coprorations.
