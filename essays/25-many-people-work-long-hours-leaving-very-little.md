## Prompt

Many people work long hours, leaving very little time with leisure activities.
Does this situation have more disadvantages or disadvantages?

## My answer

In a fast-paced contemporary world, maintaining an optimal balance between working hours and leisure activities has become a complex challenge, forcing a trade-off between financial autonomy and potential burnout. While acknowledging the potential financial demerits, I firmly believe that prioritizing one's mental and physical well-being is essential to ensure overall fulfillment in life.

First and foremost, many studies have shown that excessive overwork might lead to a wide range of adverse effects on one's wellness: working overtime takes a psychological toll on our mental well-being, while the potential financial gains will inevitably be diminished by unavoidable health-related issues in future. As financial benefits might be alluring to some, even they are not guaranteed, since the exhaustion from working overtime significantly reduces our capacity for focused, sustained work. Consequently, to counteract that negative outcome, people must set aside sufficient time to rest, thereby preventing accumulated stress from spiraling out of control.

Conversely, opponents of this trend put forward a compelling argument, claiming that in the modern era of economic deprivation, it is vital to secure one's financial stability. As the cost of living has been skyrocketing, while wages remained stagnant, the decision of spending extra hours at work consitutes a necessity for some individuals to keep their households afloat. On top of that, many people gravite toward the notion of building a financial cushion early on, which often requires working extra hours, thereby securing stable future and retirement.

To conclude, I maintain that despite some potential reduction in income, both physical and mental wellness must be prioritized. The adverse effects of constantly working overtime at the cost of leisure activities completely eclipses the minor merits of such a lifestyle.
