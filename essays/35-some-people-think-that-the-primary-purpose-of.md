## Prompt

Some people think that the primary purpose of museums and art galleries is to entertain visitors, while others believe that they should focus on education and preserving cultural heritage. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

## My answer

In the modern era, a burgeoning segment of the population becomes less interested in art, therefore the attendance of museums and art galleries is steadily plummeting. Having said that, whether those establishments should primarily focus on an educational aspect, rather than entertaining the general public, remains a subject of intense debate. While acknowledging the importance of preserving cultural heritage, I firmly believe that it is essential to draw larger audiences by alluring them with the entertaining sides of such visits.

First and foremost, proponents of the entertaining approach draw attention to the dwindling number of museum visitors, as such institutions have largely become an anachronism for the general public. In light of the above, it's vital to reinvigorate the decaying interest: art galleries can shift toward more modern art by highlighting the most innovative pieces, reflecting the contemporary events. At the same time, museums could utilize social media and online platforms to reach younger audiences with the intent to advertise some of their exhibitions in a creative way. All those mentioned means can attract more people, invoke genuine interest in some, and bring in additional sources of income.

Conversely, opponents posit emphasize the potential adverse effects of such mass visiting, claiming that the constant exposure of paintings results in the faster decaying of precious exhibits; thus, the preservation of cultural heritage and scientific value are the most vital priorities. On top of that, some claim that it is fundamentally flawed to put art in the category of widely accessible entertainment: precious medieval paintings bear little resemblance to modern Hollywood movies; therefore, the access to such important entities should be granted to a small subset of determined scientists.

To conclude, I am convinced that attracting larger audiences ultimately yields significant benefits overall, ranging from introducing more people to the beauty of art to bringing in additional revenue.
