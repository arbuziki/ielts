## Prompt

News stories on TV and in newspapers are very often accompanied by pictures.
Some people say that these pictures are more effective than words.
What is your opinion about this?

## My answer

In a contemporary world, news outlets constantly evolve to keep the audience engaged. One of many effective techniques to instantly catch our attention is to augment articles with bright images. In this discourse, I will examine whether images are indeed more effective to convey ideas and their prevalence is justified.

First and foremost, a number of studies have shown that people are predisposed to perceive the easiest forms of information. This is almost subconscious for us to pick up on images rather that texts, since pictures are easier for our brains to consume. This means that an article enriched with images will stand out among the patches of bland text, which is largely capitalized on by the ovherwhelming majority of news outlets. On top of that, I believe that we have already reached the point where all newspapers are totally superseded by other types of information like images and videos.

On the other side, the opponents of abandoning texts in favor of images put forward a compelling argument, claiming that this trend has far-reaching implications for our overall cognitive abilities. By opting for the easiest sources of information like images, we condition ourselves to put the least amount of effort which eventually may diminish our intellectual capacity.

To conclude, I have a firm stance on this matter. I believe that newspapers will soon become an anachronism, while texts are being widely replaced with other types of infromation like images and videos. This shift shows no sign of slowing down, on the contrary, I expect that it will only gain the momentum going forward.
