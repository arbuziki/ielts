## Prompt

WRITING TASK 2

You should spend no more than 40 minutes on this task.

As part of a class assignment you have to write about the following topic.

Millions of people every year move to English-speaking countries such as Australia, Britain or America, in order to study at school, college or university.

Why do so many people want to study in English?
Why is English such an important international language?

Give reasons for your answer.

You should write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, English has become deeply rooted in the underlying processes of the global economy. As the skill of speaking this language has becobe inextricably intertwined with international career opportunities, many parents have begun to enroll their children in foreign educational establishments with the intent to kill two birds with one stone: teach English and boost future career. In this discourse, I will outline the root cause of this phenomenon and shed some light on the importance of English in the contemporary world.

First and foremost, it is abundantly clear that the overwhelming majority of English-speaking countries provides higher standards of living, solid employment opportunities, and overall better quality of life; therefore, many citizens from developing non-English countries naturally seek options for immigration to such prosperous destinations. One of the easiest options available is graduating from a foreign educational institution, as Western governments usually offer diverse immigration programs leveled at those with international diplomas issued by English-speaking countries. On top of that, as global disposable incomes rise, traveling overseas has become largely accessible to the general public; thus, many people strive to improve their English skills to communicate with people around the world.

In light of the above, it is obvious why English, being the bedrock of the modern world, has gained an immense traction. As the Western nations successfully exert their dominance over the vast majority of the world through exporting threir culture, values, and technologies, English has become the most widely spoken language in the world. As a matter of fact, many governments put forward various initiatives to foster the adoption of English in their countries, thereby facilitating their presence in the international economy: for instance, engineers study English to communicate with each other, tech specialists develop reading skills to understand documentation for Western-made equipment.

In conclusion, I would like to emphasize the importance of both English language and English-based education in the modern world: having such important assets at one's disposal largely boosts career opportunities and immigration options.
