## Prompt

Many countries are now introducing a four-day work week. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?

## My answer

In the modern era, maintaining an optimal balance between private domain and career aspirations has become a complex challenge. While acknowledging the potential initial negative impact on the economy, I posit that the merits of a four-day work week massively outweigh in the long-term.

First and foremost, various groups of researchers have conducted experiments, which independendly have proven that a shorter work week immensely increases the overal satisfaction among employees with their workplace: many workers claim that reduced worktime positively affects their mental well-being, allowing to set aside more time to catch up with family and pursue personal hobbies. Moreover, some research has even indicated increased productivity over larger time spans, as employees were more relaxed and motivated; thus, their increased capacity for prolonged, sustained work yielded substantial efficiency gains. As a matter of fact, recent polls have shown that many employees that are on a strict 9 to 5 schedule can be disengaged from their work by taking longer breaks, chatting with their colleagues, or merely being idle twiddling their thumbs, as people call it.

Conversely, opponents draw attention to economic stagnation across many nations, claiming that nations simply cannot afford experimenting with shorter work schedules, since productivity is strictly associated with the amount of committed work time. On top of that, as four-day work week bears little resemblance to dedicated hard work, some of high-ranking executives cast a shadow of a doubt on the reseach, thereby denying potential long-term advantages of such a work schedule.

To conclude, I firmly believe that the wide acceptance of a four-day work week is a matter of time, as it carries substantial benefits to both employees' mental well-being and company's productivity.
