## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Being a celebrity - such as a famous film star or sports personality - brings problems as well as benefits.

Do you think that being a celebrity brings more benefits or more problems?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, it is becoming increasingly easier to become famous due to the prevalence of social networks, streaming platforms, and other online services that allow to embrace large audiences. While gaining prominence yields substantial benefits, at the same time, it comes at a cost of being under constant pressure. In this discource I will try to examine the merits and demerits of being a prominent figure, wherther a movie star or a videobloger.

First and foremost, it is the financial aspect that attracts people from all walks of life to seek prominence by all means necessary. Indeed, many of us are secretly dreaming of striking it rich and shining in the limelight. The opportunity to never work again is very appealing to many, considering the contemporary time when a significant number of people lives paycheck to paycheck.

Having said that, it is undeniable that being a celebrity takes an immense psychological toll on mental well-being. Being under constant surveillance of paparrazi consigns individuals to a state of incessant anxiety and a profound incapacity for living regular life.

In my own experience, when I was a teenager, I was a huge fan of Michael Jackson and was paying close attention to his personal life and career. It was a stark realization to observe his life eventually spirale out of control, despite him being one of the most famous celebrities once. On top of that, he was devastated of not being able to have a normal childhood like his friends.

To conclude, I think that being a celebrity generally offsets all the disadvantages that it might come with.
