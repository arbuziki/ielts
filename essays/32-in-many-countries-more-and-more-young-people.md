## Prompt

In many countries, more and more young people are unable to find jobs after graduation. What problems do you think youth unemployment will cause to the individual and society? What can be done to cope with this problem?

## My answer

In a contemporary world, the unemployment rates among young specialists have skyrocketed, which has surfaced as a formidable challenge confronting modern nations. As many young graduates face an acute shortage of empoyment opportunities on the labor market, many people draw the government's attention to this problem, pushing it to take decisive action. In this discourse, I will shed light on the repercussions of this trend on society, while providing potential solutions to clamp down on this phenomenon.

First and foremost, being unable to find a fitting role after graduation undermines both the financial stability and the mental well-being of younger generations, which ultimately places an immense strain on the whole nation. As unemployment rises, countries face the surging rates of juvenile delinquency and criminal activity, thereby exacerbating the perennial economic struggles. Last but not least, the struggle of being unable to seek a job takes a massive psychological toll on young individuals, which might result in severe depressions or other health-related problems.

Consequently, in order to counteract these pressures, the government must step in and introduce diverse initiatives with the intent of creating new work positions. For instance, the authorities could stimulate the economic growth by drawing foreign investments or landing international corporations; thus, in the long-term, such initiatives will facilitate the formation of new jobs. On top of that, state authorities could subsidize businesses for hiring young employees and provide tax reductions for this segment of the workforce.

To conclude, I firmy believe that the trend in question adversely impacts societies and compromises economies across many nations, as solving this issue requires diligent effort and legislative intervention.
