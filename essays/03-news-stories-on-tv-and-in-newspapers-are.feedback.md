**Verdict.** The vocabulary and grammar are close to S10 Band 7. The argument is weaker: the conclusion predicts that newspapers will disappear instead of saying whether pictures are more effective. Body 2's point about harm to our thinking is never weighed against Body 1, and the "firm stance" never answers the question. This fits the TR 6 feature "conclusions unclear/unjustified". - **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphs are clear, but the linkers are stock phrases ("First and foremost", "On top of that", "On the other side"), and the conclusion does not follow from the body. That is CC 6.

- "This is almost subconscious for us to pick up on images rather that texts" → "It is almost subconscious for us to pick up on images rather than text"
