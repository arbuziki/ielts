**Verdict.** This is a solid Band 7 and one of your best-structured essays. Both questions are answered, your position is in the introduction and repeated in the conclusion, and the body paragraphs are balanced (83 and 105 words). That is the TR fix from your practice plan, working. Compared with the S5 2A Band 8, the support is still general and some word choices are imprecise, and 7 of 12 sentences are error-free (58%, below the 70% that Band 8 needs).

- **Task Response:** All parts are addressed with two problems and two solutions, and the position is clear throughout. The support is over-generalized (TR 7): there is no specific example such as a closed school or clinic, or a named program. The solutions also don't connect to your second problem: nothing fixes the aging population or the budget strain. And "undermines small communities" is then supported only with budget figures.
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** Paragraphing and progression are clear, and the referencing works well ("the phenomenon in question", "such a trend", "it"). However, "Admittedly" introduces a concession when the sentence actually adds a cause. "…by authorities, as they should put forward…" uses "as" with no causal logic. That gives CC 7.
- **Lexical Resource:** You use good, precise items: "strips small towns of the workforce", "self-perpetuating loop", "reallocate substantial funds". There are also slips: "diligent effort … from the government", "sufficient stimulus for young people" (*incentives*), "repercussions on" (*for*), and "young specialists" twice (*young professionals / graduates*). "Incentivise" appears four times, and there is one spelling error (*competetive*). That gives LR 7.
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** The range is wide: participle clauses, colon structures, gerund subjects, "ranging from … to". 7 of 12 sentences are error-free. The errors are article use (×3), subject–verb agreement ("options is"), adverb word order and a weak participle opening. That gives GRA 7.

**Key corrections**
- "small businesses rarely can offer competetive income" → "small businesses can rarely offer competitive salaries" — word order, spelling, collocation
- "Limited education options is another root cause" → "Limited educational opportunities are another root cause" — agreement
- "the effective strategy to counteract it" → "an effective way to counteract this" — article
- "must be addressed by authorities, as they should put forward comprehensive policies" → "must be addressed by the authorities, who should introduce comprehensive policies" — article, logic
- "Admittedly, small businesses…" → "Moreover, small businesses…" — linker logic
- "has many repercussions on small towns" → "has serious repercussions for small towns" — preposition

**To reach Band 7.5–8:** Fixing just the first three corrections above would bring you to 10 of 12 error-free sentences, which is enough for GRA 8 and a 7.5. Add one concrete example to each body paragraph (for instance, a village school closing for lack of students) and pair each solution with a problem you named.
