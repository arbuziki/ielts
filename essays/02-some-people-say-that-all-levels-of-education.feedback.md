**Verdict.** The language is at about the S10 Band 7 level, but the task response falls below S5 2B Band 6. The essay turns an education question into a debate about white-collar versus manual jobs, and it ends with no position. engnovate's TR 7 misses both problems.

- **Task Response:** The question is about what schools and universities teach. Most of the essay discusses the job market, AI and low pay for manual work, and primary and secondary schools never appear. "I don't have a firm stance" leaves an agree/disagree question unanswered. This fits TR 5: "development of the position is not always clear".
- **Coherence & Cohesion:** The paragraphing is logical. The linking is mechanical ("First and foremost", "Moreover", "On the other side", "On top of that"). The last point of Body 2 (blue-collar shortages) does not connect to your argument.
- **Lexical Resource:** Good range ("tailor the education system", "pervasive automation"). There is one spelling error (necessety) and some imprecise items: "merely doesn't prioritize", "rule of supply and demand" and an overstated "irrefutable fact".
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy:** 7 of 12 sentences are error-free. Conditionals and participle clauses are used well, but article omissions recur ("majority of population", "merits and demerits").

**Key corrections**
- "majority of population" → "majority of the population" — article
- "in form of a rule of supply and demand" → "in the form of the law of supply and demand"
- "drastically reduce the necessety in purely theoretical skills" → "have drastically reduced the need for purely theoretical knowledge"
- "merely doesn't prioritize" → "simply does not prioritize"
- "I don't have a firm stance on this issue." → "On balance, I partly agree: …" — give a position

**To reach Band 7:** Answer the education question directly, for example with lab work, internships or project-based learning versus memorization. Take a clear position, even a partial one, and keep it from the introduction to the conclusion.
