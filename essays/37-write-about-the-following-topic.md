## Prompt

WRITING TASK 2

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

In the past, many people had skills such as making their own clothes and doing repairs to things in the house. In many countries, nowadays, skills like these are disappearing.

Why do you think this change is happening?
How far is this situation true in your country?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern era, the notion of "repairing items yourself" is rapidly becoming an anachronism, as a burgeoning segment of the population gravitates toward disposable items with reduced longevity. While I acknowledge the unprecedented convenience of such a trend for the general public, I firmly believe that this shift poses significant threats to our independence in the long-term.

First and foremost, this phenomenon is largely attributed to the ubiquity of widely available mass-market products. While the prices of many items, from clothing to electronics, are steadily plummeting, customers nowadays can afford to simply discard a product and replace it with a new one. Moreover, this trend, known as a throwaway culture, is actively driven by manufactures, as it result in the formation of continuous flow of repeat buyers. In light of the above, many skeptics draw attention to the deeply-rooted issues of such over-reliance on another companies, emphasizing a gradual loss of immensely important skills, such as sewing or welding.

Reflecting on my personal experience, I clearly observe the same trend gaining momentum in my home country. The influx of cheap goods from our trade partners, primarily China, completely eliminates the necessity of repairing items. On top of that, there is a famous local joke about the older generation being able to fix things, while the youth has never seen a sewing machine or a soldering iron.

To conclude, despite unparalleled comfort that this approach bestows upon consumers, I maintain that the disadvantages will inevitably outweigh the comfort-related benefits, as people will ultimately no longer be able to handle even basic repairs, thereby making them highly dependent on service companies.
