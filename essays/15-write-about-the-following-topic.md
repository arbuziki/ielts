## Prompt

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write about the following topic:

Using a computer every day can have more negative than positive effects on young children.

Do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## My answer

In the modern digital era, maintaining an optimal balance between children's computer autonomy and their social life has become a challenge. While a burgeoning segment of the population maintains that parents must adapt to the digital reality of a contemporary world and accept the new computer-related habits of their kids, other claim that excessive screen time might be detrimental to children's development in the long term. Even though I acknowledge that the inappropriate use of computers can adversly impact children's development, I posit that the merits of this technology are immeasurable and cannot be overshadowed by these minor drawbacks.

First and foremost, the technology itself is incredibly groundbreaking: many researches claim that it stands alongside such milestones as the industrial revolution or the envention of the wheel. The ubiquity of computers and smart devices multiplied by the immense productivity boost gained from their usage, leaves no other option, but to embrace the techology. Computers, when used properly, can substantially improve studying by providing access to the latest information from numerous sources across the world. On top of that, specialized software can foster children's creativity and encourage critical thinking. Moreover, online education driven by the prevalence of high band-width Internet connections marked a new step in studying. Speaking of me, I managed to learn a foreign language using guides from services like YouTube and other without having to paid for private tutors.

On the other hand, reflecting on my own experience, the overwhelming majority of youth tends to use computers for entirely different purposes that bear little resemblance to the immense advantages that this technology has to offer. Precisely speaking, among the negative aftermaths that accompany the computer age are the following experiences: online videogames, social media, streaming platforms, and short video services, to name a few. As a matter of fact, engaging in such activities proves to be disruptive for the educational process by being extremely addictive and pulling the focus away from studies.

To conclude, I have a firm stance on this matter: I believe that the merits of computers in terms of their use by teenagers substantially outweigh the demerits. When used unporperly, the technology can adversly impact children's development, but it can be largely counteracted by parental guidance and supervision.
