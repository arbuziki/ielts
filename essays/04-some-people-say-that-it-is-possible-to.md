## Prompt

Some people say that it is possible to tell a lot about a person's culture and character from their choice of clothes. Do you agree or disagree?

## My answer

In the modern era, clothing has largely become an attribute of fashion and cultural affiliation, rather than a survival necessity that it used to be throughout the history. In my opinion, while it's true that many people pick certain clothes to highlight the uniqueness of their personality, the cultural importance of dresses is almost non-existent.

First and foremost, it is abundantly clear, that pervasive globalization has blurred boundaries between various cultures. Stemming from the United States' dominance, the western culture has been largely adopted by many nations across the globe, which, to some extent, has diminished our cultural identities. Thus, in a contemporary world, where everyone wears the same brands, the cultural aspect of clothing has mostly become an anachronism. As a matter of fact, I believe that the only examples of cultural-related clothing left have a religious basis, but even those rare occurences are frowned upon by many nowadays.

Having said that, there is another side of this matter that has to do with our individuality. Many people, regardless of their cultural origin, are apt to dress a certain way to stand out of the crowd. This trend, I believe, is especially noticeable among the youth, which uses clothing to send a signal about their subcultural affiliation. Many diverse subcultures are associated with a very distinct dress code; you can clearly tell if a person is a hippie, punk, emo, goth and so on.

To conclude, I maintain that while the cultural element of clothes has largely degraded, the individual aspect is still relevant.
